## Return visit

About four to six weeks after we finish, we come back. By then the heating, the ventilation and the humidity have had a chance to move the paper. That movement is settling. It is the reason for the visit.

We look at seams, edges, corners and any joinery that was covered. Touch-ups that belong to the hang are done on that visit. There is no call-out charge for this visit.

## Seams and edges

A seam that opens, or an edge that lifts, because of the way the material was hung, is our work to put right. We re-adhere, trim and touch in. We do not paint over a problem and hope the light is kind.

Natural seams in grasscloth, silk and hand-painted goods are a property of the material. We will have said so before the hang. Those are not faults. A seam that has let go is different, and we treat it as such.

## Priority repair call-out

During the guarantee, a call about our own workmanship comes ahead of new enquiries. The contact page is the quickest way. A photograph helps us see whether we are looking at the hang, a knock, or the building.

If it is the hang, we book the return as a priority. If it is the building, we will say that plainly rather than charge you for a repair that will fail again.

## Humidity and settling

Paper and textile move with the moisture in the room. A new hang will ease as it dries, and it may ease again when the heating season starts or when a closed room is opened to damp weather. That is ordinary. It is why the return visit waits four to six weeks, rather than the next morning.

Keep the heat steady and the room ventilated. A cold, damp wall, a leak above a ceiling, or a fan heater pointed at one seam is how edges lift. Do not steam-clean wallcoverings. Do not sit a humidifier against the paper.

If a stain spreads, or a wall feels cold and soft, look for damp before you look for a decorator. We would rather be called early than asked to hang the same wall twice over a wet substrate.

## Care and cleaning

This is general guidance for the materials we hang. Where the maker has issued a care note, that note leads. When a mark appears, stop. Household cleaners, steam and magic sponges are how a small mark becomes a large one.

### Silk

Dust with a clean, dry, soft cloth. No water, no solvent, no steam. A stain needs a conservator, not a detergent. Keep handbags, chair backs and sun off the cloth where you can.

### Grasscloth and natural weaves

Dry dust only, and lightly. Moisture stains grasscloth and can swell the weave. Seams and variation in colour are part of the material. Do not try to scrub them even.

### Fabric walling

Vacuum on a low suction with a soft brush head, if the maker allows it. Do not soak the textile. Spot treatment belongs to a specialist, tested somewhere that will not show.

### Vinyl and contract wallcoverings

Wipe with a barely damp cloth and a drop of mild soap, then dry. No abrasive creams, no neat bleach, no scourers. Contract vinyl will take more than silk. It will not take a careless bucket.

### Hand-painted papers

No liquids on the surface. Dust dry, and rarely. Direct sun fades a scenic paper unevenly. Marks, flaking and water damage need a paper conservator. We can help you find the right person. We will not experiment on a de Gournay or Fromental-style panel.

### Murals

Follow the maker's care note for that print or that hand-made piece. Do not use household spray cleaner. If a panel is damaged, photograph it and call us before anyone tries to patch it.

### Acoustic wallcoverings and panels

Vacuum dust from the face with low suction and a soft head. Do not soak the fabric or the perforations. Water and heavy adhesive at the edge can close the surface the panel was specified to keep open.

### How is snagging handled?

Snagging is part of the package. A sample room or sample area is agreed first, in the permanent lighting where possible, and every later room is measured against it. Then comes the hang, then snagging, then a return visit about four to six weeks after completion, at no call-out charge, when seams, edges and corners are checked once the paper has settled. Visible seams and shading in natural weaves are properties of the material, and they are settled at the sample stage rather than raised as snags. At Hyde London City the record starts with a finished sample room in November 2022.

### What goes into the O&M and handover pack?

The handover includes a room-by-room record of the installed product, colourway, batch and care sheet. It also includes spare material from the same batch, kept dry with its batch label and stored as the maker recommends, and a note of the limits of matching a later repair, since a different batch will show. The aftercare terms are included too. Fire, acoustic and environmental performance is evidenced by the exact product's own documents, accepted by the responsible project professional. The fire classification itself comes from testing and classification of the product in a stated build-up and is declared by the wallcovering manufacturer.

### Can you assess or repair existing or historic wallpaper?

For a wallcovering hung by others, send overall and close-up photographs, the product and batch if known, when the problem appeared and any recent changes, and Mr Wallcover will assess whether a repair is possible. Marks, flaking or water damage on a hand-painted paper need a paper conservator. Mr Wallcover can help find the right person, but will not experiment on a de Gournay or Fromental-style panel. The twelve-month workmanship guarantee covers Mr Wallcover's own installations and does not transfer to an existing hang by another installer.

## Twelve-month workmanship guarantee

For twelve months from completion, Mr Wallcover guarantees the workmanship of the hang. That covers seams opening, edges lifting, and adhesive failure that comes from the way we hung the material.

It does not cover building movement, damp, leaks, or a substrate that fails. It does not cover damage by later trades, impact, furniture, or cleaning the material cannot take.

About four to six weeks later we come back, once the paper has had a chance to settle. Seams and edges are looked at. For twelve months, workmanship of the hang is guaranteed. Building movement and damp are not.

Some people call this a warranty. It is the same promise: twelve months on our workmanship, and a clear line where the building takes over from the hang.

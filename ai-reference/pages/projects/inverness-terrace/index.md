## The work

A private residential building, named here by street only.

The exterior was scaffolded front and rear. Stucco and render were repaired, including cornices, ornamental capitals and architraves. Balconies and the portico, the windows, and the rear brickwork were all in the same scope. The finished scheme is a Dulux Trade off-white.

The main run was June to September 2018, busiest in September, with returns in October and December 2018.

How the exterior work was carried out

## Survey, access, repairs and finishing.

The exterior commissions at Inverness Terrace and St George's Square each covered the front and rear of a residential building. The case study records the scope, dates and finished surfaces for this project.

01

### A defined exterior scope

Both records cover a full front and rear exterior. The scope includes the building's masonry or render, architectural details and windows.

02

### Scaffolded access

The buildings were scaffolded for the exterior work. Mr Wallcover supplies and manages scaffolding as part of the exterior service; the St George's Square record includes its strike in late November 2024.

03

### Repairs before the finish

Stucco, masonry and render repairs formed part of these commissions. The individual record identifies the cornices, cracks and other areas made good before the paint finish.

04

### The architectural details

The scope extended beyond the flat elevation: cornices, windows and entrance details were included. Balconies and the portico appear in the Inverness Terrace record; columns, balustrade and railings at St George's Square.

05

### The recorded paint finish

Dulux Trade finishes are recorded on both projects. The case study describes what was used on that building; it is not a universal paint specification for a different substrate or condition.

06

### The actual programme

The dates and photographs belong to the completed commission. Inverness Terrace records return visits in October and December 2018; St George's Square records works from July to November 2024. A new project needs its own scope and programme.

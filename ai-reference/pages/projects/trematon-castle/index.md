## The castle

Trematon is a Norman motte-and-bailey castle, built in 1068 and granted to the Duchy of Cornwall in 1337. The Duchy still owns it today. A 13th-century gatehouse still guards the bailey. Within the walls, an 11,000 sq ft Georgian manor with nine bedrooms was added in the early 1800s, its windows looking out over nine acres of gardens and the estuary beyond.

The house has beautiful proportions, and it needed a lot of restoration: paint stripped back to a blank canvas, then a multi-year, foundational renovation. Trematon is also the setting of House of Hackney’s campaigns and gives its name to the TREMATONIA print.

## The brief

To hang House of Hackney wallpapers across the Georgian house. That meant large-scale murals, densely drawn florals and ceiling-to-ceiling “print drenching”, in a house where every wall is a little out of true and every ceiling a little out of level.

## Our approach

**Strip-out and preparation.** Our photographs from 2019 and early 2020 show rooms taken back to bare plaster, old finishes scraped off and the atrium stripped and lined. Reinforced lining materials (Wallrock and Anaglypta cartons appear on site in June 2019) were used to give the hand-printed papers a true, stable ground.

**Ceilings as a fifth wall.** Several rooms were papered overhead. That includes the skylit atrium, where the print had to be cut around a decorative oval lantern and a chandelier rose, and the small box room, papered on all four walls and the ceiling.

**Murals and setting-out.** PLANTASIA is a panoramic forest landscape that does not forgive an error. Each drop was set out to plan, and photographs from June 2020 show the crew working on ladders across the full height of the room.

**Pattern and seam control.** Digitally printed papers in deep, saturated colours were batch-checked from the roll labels, and pattern matches were carried across corners, chimney breasts and door heads.

**Heritage relief.** In November 2021 the team installed Lincrusta, the embossed heritage wallcovering made since 1877. Panels were laid out on the table, then hung to a laser line for perfect vertical alignment.

**Living house, long programme.** The work ran in phases between 2019 and 2022, around a family home, a working estate and a house that was opening its doors to guests.

## Outcome

A Georgian house within a Norman castle, dressed wall by wall and ceiling by ceiling in the prints of the brand that lives there, across nearly three years of phased work. For a wallcovering contractor there are few sterner tests than hanging a designer’s own paper in the designer’s own home.

How the commission ran

## The same standards, on every commission.

Each commission has a written scope by room, walls prepared until they are true, protected floors, batch and shade control, set-out from the room’s focal points, and a return visit. [Read the method](https://www.mrwallcover.com/practice/#method).

The six standards in full

01

### Survey and scope, room by room

We measure the wall that will actually be hung, allow for the repeat and the wastage, and write a scope of works room by room before anyone orders paper.

02

### Preparation decides the finish

Walls are filled, made good and primed until they are true. If a wall is not ready for the material specified, we say so before the last drop is ordered.

03

### Protection and containment

Floors, furniture and finished joinery are protected as part of the hang. Work is contained and the place is left tidy each evening. In a home we are visitors; in a hotel we work around the house.

04

### Batch and shade control

Deliveries are checked against the measure, and batches and shading are managed so that a corridor of rooms reads as one surface.

05

### Set out from the room's focal points

Pattern is set out from chimneypieces, doorcases and bed walls, so the pattern sits square to the cornice and the joinery.

06

### One team, then the return visit

The people who start are the people who finish. Four to six weeks after completion we come back to look at every seam and edge, and the workmanship is guaranteed for twelve months.

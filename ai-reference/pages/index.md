How we work

## Five steps, and the same people throughout.

[Explore the five services](https://www.mrwallcover.com/practice/#service-heading)

1. 01

### Consultation

We start with a conversation. We advise, and we listen to the date, the building, and the way you need to keep using it.

2. 02

### Scope, room by room

After the survey we estimate and write a scope of works by room. Once agreed, that scope is the template for the job. Openings come out of the measure. Pattern repeat and wastage go in.

3. 03

### A leader on the ground

Every project has a project leader who is your day-to-day contact. They look after the craft, the progress, and what you have been told to expect.

4. 04

### Weekly oversight

On a longer commission, senior oversight quality-controls the work and agrees with you, each week, what happens next.

5. 05

### The same core team

The people you meet at the start are the core team that finishes. On a long project, people can stay on site or nearby, where that suits the building and the budget.

Aftercare

## The hang is not finished when we leave.

About four to six weeks later we come back, once the paper has had a chance to settle. Seams and edges are looked at. For twelve months, workmanship of the hang is guaranteed. Building movement and damp are not.

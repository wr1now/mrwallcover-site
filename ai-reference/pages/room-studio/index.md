## From measured walls to considered finishes.

Start with measurements or trace one room on a floor plan. Set the ceiling height, add doors and windows, then try finishes wall by wall. Your wallpaper keeps its real pattern size as you adjust the room.

Upload a complete repeating tile or a measured strip repeat. Check the tiled proof before applying it. Larger walls reveal more pattern; they do not enlarge the motif. Use a sharp source image: the studio preserves your artwork rather than inventing missing detail.

The preview is illustrative. Colour and limewash movement are approximate. Test a sample in your room before ordering. The three example render packages are available here. Automatic plan reading, cloud saving and finished rendering of your own room are unavailable until the private services are configured.

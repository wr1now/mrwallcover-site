See how the pattern matches

**Free match**

There is no pattern to line up across the joins. Each length can start independently.

Illustrative · three adjacent lengths, not a cutting plan. Follow the product label.

**Reverse hang**

Alternate lengths turn through 180°. Use this only when the maker asks for it; the arrows show the hanging direction.

Illustrative · three adjacent lengths, not a cutting plan. Follow the product label.

**Straight match**

The same point in the design meets at the same height on every length.

Illustrative · three adjacent lengths, not a cutting plan. Follow the product label.

**Half-drop**

The next length starts half a repeat lower. The third length returns to the first pattern position.

Illustrative · three adjacent lengths, not a cutting plan. Follow the product label.

**Third drop**

Each next length moves a third of a repeat lower. The pattern returns to its first position on the fourth length.

Illustrative · three adjacent lengths, not a cutting plan. Follow the product label.

**Quarter drop**

Each next length moves a quarter of a repeat lower. The pattern returns to its first position on the fifth length.

Illustrative · three adjacent lengths, not a cutting plan. Follow the product label.

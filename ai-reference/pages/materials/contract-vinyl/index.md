## Care

More tolerant of cleaning than paper or silk, and still only as the maker allows. Vinyl on a wall is not, by itself, a fire certificate.

Installation experience

## On the record

At [Raffles London at The OWO](https://www.mrwallcover.com/projects/raffles-london-the-owo/), a bespoke Vescom wallcovering made for the hotel went onto walls and parts of the joinery. At [Hyde London City](https://www.mrwallcover.com/projects/old-bailey-hotel/), Timorous Beasties' Tropical Clouded Leopard and Totem Damask went into the guest rooms as custom-printed vinyl. At [Hilton London Metropole](https://www.mrwallcover.com/projects/hilton-london-metropole/), a jet-black embossed Muraspec and Vescom on conference-room panels; at the [Mercedes-AMG showroom](https://www.mrwallcover.com/projects/mercedes-amg-showroom-london/), black matt Muraspec; at [DoubleTree by Hilton London – West End](https://www.mrwallcover.com/projects/doubletree-west-end/), Tektura wide vinyl in 2017.

At [Novotel London Canary Wharf](https://www.mrwallcover.com/projects/novotel-london-canary-wharf/), concrete-effect and timber-grain wallcoverings we installed in 2016 were photographed six years on, in 2022. The [hotel specification guide](https://www.mrwallcover.com/advice/hotel-wallcovering-specification/) covers cleaning, batches and documentation.

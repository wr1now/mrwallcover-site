**Consider it when**

: Print, backing and care vary substantially between products.

**Before ordering**

## Joins

Seams are part of a hung wall. The match follows the repeat: free, straight or a half-drop. Half-drop papers and murals are set out on site; the calculator gives the order quantity, not the set-out.

## Where it belongs

Homes and hotels

03 · Installation

## Handling

The wall has to be sound first. Lining is used when the maker or the wall requires it.

04 · Living with it

## Care

Follow the maker. Many papers are not washable. We do not call a family washable, fire-rated or seamless unless that product’s own data says so.

Digital colour is a guide. Order a cutting or a sample before you commit to a room. Some makers’ own sample routes are listed under [official buying routes](https://www.mrwallcover.com/buying-routes/). We can install material you have already bought, or source a family and install it. Either way, the next step is a short note about the room.

Installation experience

## On the record

Printed papers make up most of our residential record. At the [Castle of Trematon](https://www.mrwallcover.com/projects/trematon-castle/), between 2019 and 2022, we hung seven House of Hackney papers through the Georgian house, with lining, papered ceilings and pattern-matched full-height drops. In [a listed Georgian house in North London](https://www.mrwallcover.com/projects/north-london-residence/), House of Hackney's BAMBUSA in Midnight went on every wall of a guest bedroom with sloping floors, each drop set to a laser line so the bamboo reads upright.

Relief papers belong to the same family: Anaglypta on the high ceilings at [Pendley Manor Hotel](https://www.mrwallcover.com/projects/pendley-manor-hotel/), hung first and painted afterwards, beside Cole & Son's Petroushka velvet flock, and Lincrusta relief at Trematon, aligned to a laser line. Our guide to [papering ceilings and staircases](https://www.mrwallcover.com/advice/papered-ceilings-and-staircases/) covers relief overhead and on a stair. The [choosing guide](https://www.mrwallcover.com/advice/choosing-wallcoverings/) compares paper with the other families.

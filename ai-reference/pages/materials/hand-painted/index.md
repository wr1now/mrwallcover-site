# Hand-painted scenic papers

Scenic and panelled papers, in the manner of houses such as de Gournay and Fromental. We hang them.

![de Gournay hand-painted wallpaper with brass wall lights, a private residence, London.](https://www.mrwallcover.com/media/img/r/brand-de-gournay-01-800.webp)

Installation experience

## On the record

At [Brown's Hotel, Mayfair](https://www.mrwallcover.com/projects/browns-hotel-mayfair/) the scheme includes scenic and chinoiserie wallcoverings, among them Lewis & Wood's Adam's Eden. At a [tea house in West London](https://www.mrwallcover.com/projects/tea-house-jaillon-studio/) for Jaillon Studio, the garden room is a panoramic scenic paper of a swan lake and flowering hydrangeas. We have also installed de Gournay and Fromental papers, including a large volume of Fromental at Four Seasons Ten Trinity Square.

The [scenic set-out guide](https://www.mrwallcover.com/advice/hand-painted-murals-set-out/) covers elevations, panel sequence and finished dimensions, and [hand-painted wallpaper installation](https://www.mrwallcover.com/services/hand-painted-wallpaper-installation/) explains how we hang them.

Before you choose

**Consider it when**
: One wall or a room needs a planned composition rather than a repeating pattern.

**The trade-off**
: A preview cannot confirm the real colour, texture or precise crop.

**Before ordering**
: Confirm scale, panel count, sequence, backing and care instructions.

## Care

Follow the maker. An event mural is not the same duty as a guest-room vinyl.

Digital colour is a guide. Order a cutting or a sample before you commit to a room. We can install material you have already bought, or source a family and install it. Either way, the next step is a short note about the room.

## On the record

Our published murals include Calico Wallpaper's Overture for [Lee Broom](https://www.mrwallcover.com/projects/calico-lee-broom-overture/) (2025), Estuary for [Ahluwalia at Rosewood London](https://www.mrwallcover.com/projects/calico-ahluwalia-estuary-rosewood/) (2026) and a painted landscape at [Heathrow Terminal 4](https://www.mrwallcover.com/projects/heathrow-terminal-4-calico/), hung overnight in 2018. Muraspec's extra-wide digital prints went up at the [Everyman cinema in Liverpool](https://www.mrwallcover.com/projects/everyman-liverpool/) and at [Oxygen Freejumping, The O2](https://www.mrwallcover.com/projects/oxygen-freejumping-the-o2/), and a blue gradient mural at the [SCP showroom in Pimlico](https://www.mrwallcover.com/projects/scp-showroom-pimlico/).

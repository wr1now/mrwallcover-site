Before you choose

**Consider it when**
: Natural fibre and a visible panel character are part of the look you want.

**The trade-off**
: Adjacent drops can differ in shade; do not expect an invisible joint.

**Before ordering**
: Review a full-wall example, panel layout and the exact care sheet.

## Handling

Clean hands, an adhesive that suits the backing, and enough length from one batch to keep a wall together.

04 · Living with it

## Care

Dry and gentle. Adhesive on the face marks the weave. Not a washable surface.

## On the record

At [Four Seasons Hotel London at Ten Trinity Square](https://www.mrwallcover.com/projects/four-seasons-ten-trinity-square/) we hung Phillip Jeffries Tweed and Omexco between 2016 and 2019, with shading on the natural textiles checked continuously so the walls read as a single surface. At [The Biltmore Mayfair](https://www.mrwallcover.com/projects/biltmore-mayfair/), the 2018 guest-room programme of more than 15,000 m² included a Phillip Jeffries schedule.

The [grasscloth guide](https://www.mrwallcover.com/advice/grasscloth-seams-and-variation/) explains what is normal and what is a fault, and [grasscloth installation](https://www.mrwallcover.com/services/grasscloth-installation/) explains how we set out and shade a room.

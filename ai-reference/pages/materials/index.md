### Paper and non-woven

**Setting.** Homes and hotels

**Joins.** Seams are part of a hung wall. The match follows the repeat: free, straight or a half-drop. Half-drop papers and murals are set out on site; the calculator gives the order quantity, not the set-out.

**Care.** Follow the maker. Many papers are not washable. We do not call a family washable, fire-rated or seamless unless that product’s own data says so.

### Grasscloth and natural weaves

**Setting.** Rooms where variation is wanted

**Joins.** Seams show. The direction of the weave is agreed before the first length is cut.

**Care.** Dry and gentle. Adhesive on the face marks the weave. Not a washable surface.

### Silk and textiles

**Setting.** Rooms where light is part of the finish

**Joins.** Seams are planned so the light from the window does not catch a ridge.

**Care.** Silk marks quickly. Care is the maker’s note, usually dry. It is not a corridor vinyl.

### Hand-painted scenic papers

**Setting.** Suites and drawing rooms

**Joins.** Panels follow a sequence. The scene has to meet across doors, windows and corners. The set-out starts from a fixed point.

**Care.** As the maker specifies. These are not washable contract surfaces.

### Murals

**Setting.** A single wall or a room with a scene

**Joins.** Set out from a fixed point so the image meets where it should. A roll calculator will not price a mural.

**Care.** Follow the maker. An event mural is not the same duty as a guest-room vinyl.

### Contract vinyl

**Setting.** Busy hotel rooms and corridors

**Joins.** Seams are still there. They are planned, not promised away.

**Care.** More tolerant of cleaning than paper or silk, and still only as the maker allows. Vinyl on a wall is not, by itself, a fire certificate.

### Acoustic wallcoverings

**Setting.** Rooms where sound was part of the specification

**Joins.** Edges, trims and cuts have to keep the system the way it was specified.

**Care.** Follow the system maker. We do not claim a decorative finish is an acoustic rating.

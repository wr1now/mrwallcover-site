Before you choose

**Consider it when**
: You want a textile surface whose appearance changes with the light.

**The trade-off**
: Delicate faces can be less forgiving of marks and handling.

**Before ordering**
: See the sample in the room and confirm preparation and care requirements.

## Care

Silk marks quickly. Care is the maker’s note, usually dry. It is not a corridor vinyl.

Digital colour is a guide. Order a cutting or a sample before you commit to a room. We can install material you have already bought, or source a family and install it. Either way, the next step is a short note about the room.

## On the record

At [Four Seasons Hotel London at Ten Trinity Square](https://www.mrwallcover.com/projects/four-seasons-ten-trinity-square/), the hotel wallcoverings of 2019, about 5,000 m², included Fromental silks and Dedar. At [Raffles London at The OWO](https://www.mrwallcover.com/projects/raffles-london-the-owo/), selected suites have Vescom Alcantara.

Silk shows the wall beneath it in side light, so preparation comes first; see [wall preparation](https://www.mrwallcover.com/advice/wall-preparation/) and [silk wallcovering installation](https://www.mrwallcover.com/services/silk-wallcovering-installation/).

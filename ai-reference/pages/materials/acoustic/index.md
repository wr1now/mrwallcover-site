# Acoustic wallcoverings

A textured face over an acoustic construction, or an acoustic panel.

Before you choose

**Consider it when**
: Sound performance forms part of a professionally specified system.

**The trade-off**
: A decorative texture is not evidence of acoustic performance.

**Before ordering**
: Obtain the exact system data, installation detail and project acceptance.

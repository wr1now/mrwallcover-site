# Wallcoverings on joinery and bespoke panels

Wallcoverings carried onto doors, wardrobes, panelling and furniture, with bespoke panels created for the chosen finish. Full joinery services can be arranged through our trusted, vetted specialist partners.

[Start your project](https://www.mrwallcover.com/contact/?service=wallcovering&topic=service%3Ajoinery-wallcoverings-and-panels) [See the work](https://www.mrwallcover.com/projects/)

In some rooms the finish carries beyond the wall: across doors, wardrobe fronts, panelling and bespoke furniture, so that joinery and walls read as one surface. We apply wallcoverings to joinery with the same discipline as the walls around it. Seams, edges and returns are planned at the survey so the joins disappear.

Joinery is a different surface from plaster. Faces are sealed and primed to suit the material, edges are wrapped or finished tight, and the pattern is carried across doors and frames. At [Lee Broom's Shoreditch showroom](https://www.mrwallcover.com/projects/calico-lee-broom-overture/) we ran Calico Wallpaper's Overture straight across a flush door until it vanished into the drape. At [Raffles London at The OWO](https://www.mrwallcover.com/projects/raffles-london-the-owo/) the wallcovering continued from the walls onto parts of the joinery.

Where a panel, door or carcass needs a new surface rather than paper, we wrap it in specialist architectural film such as Cover Styl', in wood, stone, metal, leather-look and solid finishes. Where film is not the right answer, we finish panels in paper, fabric or relief wallcovering instead. See [architectural and furniture film wrapping](https://www.mrwallcover.com/services/architectural-film-wrapping/) for film on existing joinery.

When the finish a designer wants cannot be bought off the shelf, we create bespoke panels for joinery: framed and shaped wall panels, headboard panels, and panels for door and wardrobe faces, made to the drawing and the finish. Full joinery work can be arranged through trusted, vetted specialist partners. Heritage relief can also form part of the finish. At the [Castle of Trematon](https://www.mrwallcover.com/projects/trematon-castle/) we hung Lincrusta relief panels to a laser line.

Mr Wallcover is a wallcovering specialist, not a joinery company. We develop the panel and wallcovering details with the designer, agree a sample in the room's own light, then create and finish the panels and install the wallcoverings. Where full joinery services are needed, we coordinate our work with trusted, vetted specialist partners, followed by the same [aftercare](https://www.mrwallcover.com/aftercare/) for our installation.

Trained at Solar Screen's headquarters in Luxembourg in window films (Solar Screen) and architectural and furniture wrapping film (Cover Styl'). We install to the maker's own instructions.

Makers: [Cover Styl' architectural and furniture films](https://coverstyl.com/en/), [Lincrusta heritage relief](https://www.lincrusta.com/)

## Doors papered in

A flush door in a papered wall can disappear when the pattern is carried across its face and trimmed to its edges. At the [SCP showroom in Pimlico](https://www.mrwallcover.com/projects/scp-showroom-pimlico/), a full-height flush door was papered in with the gilded marbled wallcovering so that it almost vanishes into the marbling. At [Lee Broom's Shoreditch showroom](https://www.mrwallcover.com/projects/calico-lee-broom-overture/), Calico Wallpaper's Overture, a trompe-l'oeil theatre curtain, runs straight across a flush door in the back wall, the pattern carried over the door so that it disappears into the drape. The [set-out and pattern matching guide](https://www.mrwallcover.com/advice/set-out-and-pattern-matching/) explains how a pattern is kept continuous across a break like this.

## Panels wrapped one by one

On panelled walls each panel is its own small wall. At [Hilton London Metropole](https://www.mrwallcover.com/projects/hilton-london-metropole/), the wall panels of a large conference room were covered in Vescom panel by panel, each wrapped so the wallcovering runs the same way on every one and the edges meet on a clean line, because the room is seen all at once from the doors. At [Huntsman, Savile Row](https://www.mrwallcover.com/projects/huntsman-savile-row/), freestanding display boards were covered in the house's bespoke tweed print: four edges and two faces on each board, with the pattern square on every one.

At [Jumeirah Carlton Tower](https://www.mrwallcover.com/projects/jumeirah-carlton-tower/), where we installed wallcoverings for eight months during the renovation, Jumeirah describes the bedrooms as light oak panelling with textured wallcoverings set into the panels. Inset work leaves nowhere to hide: every edge meets a moulding or a frame.

## Edges that stay true

Where an edge will not come true, we stop asking the paper to make it. In [a prime residence in central London](https://www.mrwallcover.com/projects/imperfect-arches-external-corner-trim/), an arch that was not true was given a specialist external-corner profile, bent to the curve and fixed, with the paper scribed into its return, so the edge reads as one crisp line and is protected from wear. At the [Mercedes-AMG showroom](https://www.mrwallcover.com/projects/mercedes-amg-showroom-london/), black matt Muraspec was cut to meet narrow brushed stainless-steel trims at the doors and above the skirtings on one continuous line.

### Can wallpaper be applied to doors and wardrobes?

Yes, on sound, smooth joinery that has been sealed and primed for it. The pattern is set out across the doors so it reads as one surface when they are closed, and hinge edges, handles and returns are detailed at the survey.

### Can you create panels to match a specific finish?

Yes. We create bespoke panels for joinery to the drawing, then finish them in wallcovering, film or relief. The finish is designed in from the start and agreed on a sample before the panels are made. Full joinery services can be arranged through trusted, vetted specialist partners.

### Film or wallcovering on joinery: which is better?

Film suits hard-wearing, wipe-clean surfaces in busy areas: hotel bedroom doors, wardrobes, lifts and counters. Wallcovering suits pattern, texture and continuity with the walls. Where both could work, we sample both in the room.

### Who handles the panels, joinery and wallcovering installation?

Mr Wallcover is a wallcovering specialist, not a joinery company. We create and finish panels for joinery. For full joinery services, we can work with trusted, vetted specialist partners, coordinating the wallcovering details, installation and aftercare with their work.

### How do you price, and what does it cost?

There is no flat rate per roll. The quotation comes from a site survey or from drawings. It reflects the measured area after openings are taken off, the pattern repeat and the wastage it creates (typically 15–30%, with staircases around 30%), the state of the walls, the material, and access and protection. Access, protection and making good are written into the scope rather than discovered at the end, and the quotation says what has been allowed for. The site publishes no prices.

### Do you issue RAMS, and who supervises the site?

Risk assessments and method statements (RAMS) are issued for every package, and Dorin Burcus personally supervises the site.

### Do you take on single rooms, or only larger projects?

We focus on whole residences and hotel packages, and will take on one- or two-room commissions. The minimum installation commission is one room.

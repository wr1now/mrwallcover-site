# Mural installation

Printed and hand-made murals for houses, hotels and shops in London.

[Request a quotation](https://www.mrwallcover.com/contact/?service=wallcovering&topic=service%3Amural-installation) [See the work](https://www.mrwallcover.com/projects/)

Murals are set out from a fixed point so the scene meets where it should, including around doors and corners. Panels are checked against the supplier's layout before hanging.

Some murals are specified with a lining paper and some without. We follow the specification, and we say at the survey if the wall needs work first.

Measurements for a bespoke print are taken on site so the artwork is sized to the wall that will actually be hung.

## Measured to the wall that will be hung

A bespoke mural is printed or painted to the dimensions you supply, so we measure on site, take out the openings and give the supplier the finished dimensions of the wall that will actually be hung. For [Lee Broom's Shoreditch showroom](https://www.mrwallcover.com/projects/calico-lee-broom-overture/), we surveyed in July 2025 for Calico Wallpaper's panel layout and installed on 9 September 2025. At [Heathrow Terminal 4](https://www.mrwallcover.com/projects/heathrow-terminal-4-calico/), Calico's painted landscape was printed to the store's exact wall sizes and wrapped continuously around the corners.

Bring the installer in before the dimensions go to production. A digital print can sometimes be adjusted in the file before printing; a hand-made set cannot be stretched once it exists. The [scenic set-out guide](https://www.mrwallcover.com/advice/hand-painted-murals-set-out/) explains why.

02

## A picture, not a pattern

A mural is one image cut into drops or panels, and each piece has its own place in the picture. Panels are checked against the supplier's layout before hanging, then hung in sequence and matched across every joint. At the [Everyman cinema in Liverpool](https://www.mrwallcover.com/projects/everyman-liverpool/), Muraspec's extra-wide film and music prints were hung as single pictures from floor to ceiling, from the top of the wall down. At Heathrow the horizon had to run level and unbroken across each join and around every corner.

A gradient has no pattern to hide a join: at the [SCP showroom in Pimlico](https://www.mrwallcover.com/projects/scp-showroom-pimlico/) the blue mural was hung in order and butted exactly, so the fade lined up from panel to panel. Wide vinyl can be overlapped and cut through both layers at once, as at [Oxygen Freejumping, The O2](https://www.mrwallcover.com/projects/oxygen-freejumping-the-o2/), so fine lines run unbroken across the joint.

## Preparing the wall

A large print shows every bump beneath it. At Everyman the walls were prepared and lined before the murals went up, with scaffold towers and platforms for the tallest walls. Some murals are specified with a lining paper and some without; we follow the specification, and we say at the survey if the wall needs work first.

## Fixed dates and live venues

Event murals are hung to a date that does not move. For Ahluwalia and Calico Wallpaper, the [Estuary mural at Rosewood London](https://www.mrwallcover.com/projects/calico-ahluwalia-estuary-rosewood/) was installed in a single day, on 18 September 2026, the day before the show. At Heathrow, the mural was hung overnight on 24–25 September 2018 in a live terminal, by vetted operatives with airside passes, and the finished walls were photographed between 02:30 and 03:42.

At Everyman, the installation ran from October to December 2018, in the weeks before the cinema opened on 18 December.

## Asked about Mural installation

Illustrative material studies

### Will you measure the wall for a bespoke mural?

Yes. We measure on site, take out the openings and give the supplier the dimensions of the wall that will actually be hung.

### What lead time do you need, and how far ahead should we book?

A start date is given after the survey. If the wallcovering is free-issue, the supplier's lead time sits inside the programme, and the hang is planned around the delivery. For a scenic paper or mural, bring the installer in before the dimensions go to production, because the panels are made to the finished walls. On a tender, the tender deadline is not the requested start date: both should be stated. Lead times depend on the project. When a client or contractor needs support quickly, the team can sometimes mobilise within two to three days.

### Who is responsible if free-issue material is faulty, short or damaged?

Free-issue deliveries are checked against the measure when they arrive. Scenic panels are checked dry against the layout before any paste and before anything is cut, so a sequence, sizing or print problem comes to light while it is still easy to resolve. Quantities are ordered to cover the repeat and the wastage, typically 15–30% and around 30% on staircases, so that the last wall doesn't wait. The specification should say who orders, who receives and checks the delivery, and who approves the batch. The twelve-month guarantee covers the workmanship of the hang: seams opening, edges lifting and adhesive failure that comes from the way the material was hung. Rolls are inspected as part of preparation, although on large volumes not every roll can be opened in advance. Any defect is reported straight away to the designer, the contractor and the manufacturer. If a fault comes from the installation, Mr Wallcover covers the cost of the material.

### How do you price, and what does it cost?

There is no flat rate per roll. The quotation comes from a site survey or from drawings. It reflects the measured area after openings are taken off, the pattern repeat and the wastage it creates (typically 15–30%, with staircases around 30%), the state of the walls, the material, and access and protection. Access, protection and making good are written into the scope rather than discovered at the end, and the quotation says what has been allowed for. The site publishes no prices.

### Can you work nights or out of hours?

Out-of-hours work is on the published record. At Heathrow Terminal 4, a bespoke Calico Wallpaper mural was hung overnight on 24–25 September 2018 in a live terminal, and the finished walls were photographed between 02:30 and 03:42. In an occupied hotel, the permitted working hours, delivery routes, guest routes and daily handover are agreed for each project and written into the scope. Most of the hotel work on record was done in buildings that stayed open. Night shifts, out-of-hours and weekend work are offered, including in live hotels.

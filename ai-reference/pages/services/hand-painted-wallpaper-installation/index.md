## Planned before the panels are made

A hand-painted or panoramic paper is a composition, not a repeating pattern. The panels are made to the elevations, so the plan comes before production: each wall is drawn with its finished width and height and the position of every door, window, socket, radiator, cornice and piece of joinery, and the focal point is agreed. That is often the bed, the chimneypiece or the dining table rather than the middle of the wall.

We measure on site, take out the openings and give the supplier the finished dimensions of the wall that will actually be hung. If the room is still being built or re-plastered, bring the installer and the maker in before the dimensions go to production: skirting height, cornice depth and a new lining all change the finished wall. The [scenic set-out guide](https://www.mrwallcover.com/advice/hand-painted-murals-set-out/) explains each step.

## The sequence is the artwork

Scenic papers arrive as numbered panels, hung in the supplied order. On a continuous wall the sequence is not changed to move a motif; only a break in the wall, such as a door or a window, gives the chance to alter it. The delivered panels are checked dry against the maker's layout before any paste and before anything is cut, while a sequence or sizing problem is still easy to resolve. Our [set-out and pattern matching guide](https://www.mrwallcover.com/advice/set-out-and-pattern-matching/) covers the wider set-out.

A hand-painted set cannot be stretched to fit. Its panels are made at a fixed width, and forcing a pasted panel to close a gap stretches it and leads to shrinkage as it dries. Where a door, a window or a corner takes part of the picture, we agree where the loss is least painful: behind a door that stands open, above a window, or in a corner that furniture hides.

03

## The wall beneath a scenic paper

A scenic paper is usually smooth and lit from the side, so the wall has to be sound and true. Some papers are hung over a lining and some are specified without one; de Gournay's instructions, for example, ask for every wall to be lined with plain white lining paper. Where a lining is used, its joints never coincide with the joints of the panels, and it is dry before the panels go on.

New plaster and very absorbent walls are sized as the maker's instruction says, never sealed with neat PVA. Filling, lining and drying time are planned into the programme rather than discovered on the day the panels arrive. The [wall preparation guide](https://www.mrwallcover.com/advice/wall-preparation/) sets out what is checked.

## Existing and historic papers

For a paper hung by others, send overall and close-up photographs, the product and batch if known, when the problem appeared and any recent changes, and we will say whether a repair is possible. Marks, flaking or water damage on a hand-painted paper need a paper conservator. We can help find the right person, but we will not experiment on a hand-painted panel.

Our twelve-month workmanship guarantee covers our own installations; it does not transfer to an existing hang by another installer.

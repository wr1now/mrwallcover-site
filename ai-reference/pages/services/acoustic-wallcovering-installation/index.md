## Installed as the system was specified

An acoustic wallcovering is a textured face over an acoustic construction, or an acoustic panel. Its performance belongs to the system, so adhesive, trimming, edges and cuts follow the instruction for that system, and nothing is changed on site that would alter what was specified.

We do not claim that a decorative finish carries an acoustic rating. A decorative texture is not evidence of acoustic performance.

02

## Evidence and handover

Acoustic performance, like fire and environmental performance, is evidenced by the exact product's own documents, accepted by the responsible project professional. Before ordering, obtain the system data and the installation detail, and confirm the project's acceptance. The handover then records the installed product, colourway and batch room by room, with spare material from the same batch. The [designer specification checklist](https://www.mrwallcover.com/advice/designer-specification-checklist/) lists the fields.

### Where are acoustic wallcoverings used?

Commonly in hotel corridors and function rooms, restaurants, offices, and in homes where a room needs to be quieter. We install to the maker's specification.

### Who is responsible if free-issue material is faulty, short or damaged?

Free-issue deliveries are checked against the measure when they arrive. Scenic panels are checked dry against the layout before any paste and before anything is cut, so a sequence, sizing or print problem comes to light while it is still easy to resolve. Quantities are ordered to cover the repeat and the wastage, typically 15–30% and around 30% on staircases, so that the last wall doesn't wait. The specification should say who orders, who receives and checks the delivery, and who approves the batch. The twelve-month guarantee covers the workmanship of the hang: seams opening, edges lifting and adhesive failure that comes from the way the material was hung. Rolls are inspected as part of preparation, although on large volumes not every roll can be opened in advance. Any defect is reported straight away to the designer, the contractor and the manufacturer. If a fault comes from the installation, Mr Wallcover covers the cost of the material.

### How do you price, and what does it cost?

There is no flat rate per roll. The quotation comes from a site survey or from drawings. It reflects the measured area after openings are taken off, the pattern repeat and the wastage it creates (typically 15–30%, with staircases around 30%), the state of the walls, the material, and access and protection. Access, protection and making good are written into the scope rather than discovered at the end, and the quotation says what has been allowed for. The site publishes no prices.

### What goes into the O&M and handover pack?

The handover includes a room-by-room record of the installed product, colourway, batch and care sheet. It also includes spare material from the same batch, kept dry with its batch label and stored as the maker recommends, and a note of the limits of matching a later repair, since a different batch will show. The aftercare terms are included too. Fire, acoustic and environmental performance is evidenced by the exact product's own documents, accepted by the responsible project professional. The fire classification itself comes from testing and classification of the product in a stated build-up and is declared by the wallcovering manufacturer.

### What aftercare and guarantee are included?

Aftercare is part of every commission: a return visit about four to six weeks after completion, a priority repair call-out and a twelve-month workmanship guarantee.

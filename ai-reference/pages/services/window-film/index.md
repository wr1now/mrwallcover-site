# Window film: decorative, privacy, solar and safety

Films applied to existing glass: frosted and decorative designs, privacy, solar control, UV protection, acoustic and safety films for London homes, hotels, offices and shopfronts.

[Start your project](https://www.mrwallcover.com/contact/?service=window-film&topic=service%3Awindow-film) [See the work](https://www.mrwallcover.com/projects/)

Window film changes how existing glass behaves without replacing it. There are four families. Decorative films give a frosted, etched, gradient or patterned finish, including logos and manifestations. Privacy films screen a view by day, or hide a room completely. Solar control films reduce heat gain and glare. Safety and security films help hold broken glass together.

The right film depends on the glass, the frame, the orientation and what the room needs. Some films are not suitable for every type of double glazing, so we check the glass and the film maker's guidance before we recommend one.

Glass is cleaned to a high standard, the film is cut to the visible pane and squeegeed out, and edges are finished neatly to the frame. Small moisture haze is normal for a short time while the adhesive cures, and we explain what to expect on the day.

Trained at Solar Screen's headquarters in Luxembourg in window films (Solar Screen) and architectural and furniture wrapping film (Cover Styl'). We install to the maker's instructions. At Hyde London City (the Old Bailey Hotel) we fitted Solar Screen film to the glazing as part of the full decorating package. The same practice surveys, supplies the film if you need it, installs and comes back for aftercare.

## Six jobs a film can do

Solar control: takes heat and glare off south- and west-facing rooms, so blinds can stay open and screens stay readable.

UV protection: filters most of the ultraviolet that fades fabrics, timber floors, artwork and, in our trade, wallcoverings. Clear grades leave the view and the light almost unchanged.

Acoustic: laminating films add mass and damping to a pane and take the edge off traffic and street noise in bedrooms and meeting rooms. Film improves what is there; where the noise is severe, secondary glazing is the bigger step, and we say so at the survey.

Privacy: frosted, gradient, one-way-by-day and switchable-look films screen meeting rooms, bathrooms and street-level windows without losing daylight.

Safety and security: clear films that help hold broken glass together, for glass at low level, in doors and in places where a break would be dangerous.

Decorative: etched-look bands, patterns, manifestations and logos cut to the pane.

## How a window film job runs

The survey looks at the glass, the frame, the orientation and what the room needs, and the film maker's guidance decides what can be applied to that glazing. On the day, the glass is cleaned, the film is cut to the visible pane and squeegeed out, and the edges are finished to the frame. A slight haze while the adhesive cures is normal, and we explain what to expect.

Risk assessments and method statements (RAMS) are issued for every package, and Dorin Burcus personally supervises the site. Film can be part of a wider decorating commission, as at Hyde London City, or a job on its own.

Sample: on larger jobs a sample pane is fitted first, so the look and the light can be approved in the room.

In hotels and offices we work room by room or floor by floor, out of hours where the building needs it.

Aftercare: a short haze while the adhesive cures is normal. We explain the cleaning instructions on the day and come back if anything needs attention.

### What should I send for a window film quotation?

Tell us where the glazing is and whether you need decorative, privacy, solar control or safety film. The glazing type, frames and orientation need to be checked against the film maker's guidance before a product is recommended. Include whether you need film supplied as well as installed.

### Which type of window film do I need?

Decorative or frosted film for screening and design, privacy film to hide a view, solar control film for heat and glare, and safety or security film to help hold broken glass together. Some films combine more than one job. We recommend one after seeing the glass and the room.

### Can window film go on double glazing?

Often, but not every film suits every unit. Solar films in particular can raise the temperature of the glass. We check the glazing type and follow the film maker's guidance on what can be applied.

### Can window film be removed later?

Yes. Film can be taken off and the glass cleaned, which makes it useful for rented premises, changing branding or a room that changes use.

### Does window film soundproof a window?

It reduces noise rather than removing it. Acoustic laminating films damp the glass and soften traffic and street noise. For heavy noise, secondary glazing does more, and we tell you that at the survey rather than oversell the film.

### Will UV film stop wallpaper and furniture fading?

It removes most of the ultraviolet that drives fading, which slows it a great deal. Visible light and heat also play a part, so a solar grade can be combined where a room gets strong sun.

### Can you film a hotel or office that stays open?

Yes. At Hyde London City every window was filmed as part of the room-by-room decorating package, and at Gray's Inn Square offices were done floor by floor. We plan the order with the building team and can work out of hours.

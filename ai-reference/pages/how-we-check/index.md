# Product facts and corrections

By [Dorin Burcus](https://www.mrwallcover.com/about/#dorin), founder · Last updated 17 October 2026

Where the product facts on this site come from, how we keep a maker's documents apart from our own installation experience, and how to report a correction.

[Report a correction](https://www.mrwallcover.com/contact/?intent=advice) [See the work](https://www.mrwallcover.com/projects/)

## The maker’s instruction comes first

The maker’s current instructions for the exact product come first. Our project accounts describe work we have carried out; they do not replace the maker’s instructions for another product or wall. If the instructions and the proposed wall preparation do not agree, resolve that before ordering or hanging.

## Product references need a source and a date

- For a new product reference, we record the fact as the maker prints it, and link the document it comes from: a data sheet, hanging instructions or the product page.
- The reference must give the source link and date that document was checked. Makers change their documents, so the date tells you how current our copy is.
- Where the document is silent, we write “not stated”. We do not fill the gap from another product, another colourway or the brand as a whole.

## Checked twice

New product and buying-route references are checked against the maker’s own document, then reviewed by a person before they are published.

A reference that has not had that review stays unpublished.

This page sets the rule for those new references; it does not claim that every older page has received a second independent review. No maker or retailer directory records are being released with this buying guide.

## Words we do not use without a document

We do not call a product fire-rated, washable or waterproof unless the maker’s own document says so for that exact product, and then we use the maker’s words. A stockist listing proves that a maker lists a seller; it does not by itself prove an authorised, approved or exclusive relationship. Those labels require the maker’s explicit wording.

## Money

- No maker or seller pays to appear on this site, or to be listed higher.
- There are no affiliate links. Paid placement and commission must not determine inclusion or ordering.
- Where makers or sellers are listed, the order never depends on payment or commission.
- We do not show wallpaper prices, because they change. Where a price matters, we link to the seller’s site. The [quantity guide](https://www.mrwallcover.com/advice/quantities/) explains its estimator’s limits; it does not quote installation work.

## Corrections

If something here is wrong or out of date, tell us which page and what the maker’s document says. Corrections to new references must update the check date. Until a disputed fact is resolved, it should be withheld or clearly marked as unresolved.

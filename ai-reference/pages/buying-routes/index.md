# The right way in.

A sample request, showroom contact and wallpaper order are different things. Start with the maker’s official route and check who can buy, where it can be delivered and which terms apply.

No paid placement, affiliate ranking or individual seller endorsement. Country selectors and the maker’s current product and destination terms take precedence.

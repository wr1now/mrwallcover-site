About

# Dorin Burcus

Founder

Dorin Burcus founded Mr Wallcover. He has worked in the trade since 2012, starting as a labourer and going on to run wallcovering packages in prime London hotels.

The earliest commissions dated on this site are Four Seasons Hotel London at Ten Trinity Square, 2016 to 2019, guest room and suite wallpapering, and Novotel London Canary Wharf in 2016. Since then the public work includes The OWO on Whitehall, Brown's Hotel in Mayfair, hotels in Holborn and at Silverstone, and The Lanesborough.

The practice is based in London, covers London and the surrounding areas, and works UK-wide for selected projects. Every job is planned around the building and the people who have to keep using it, so that the disruption stays small and the finish does not.

What you see on this site is public. Private clients are not named.

How the work is carried

## Three things we will not trade away.

### Respect

In a home, the building is someone's house and we are temporary visitors. The work is planned around the household, kept physically contained, and left tidy. A hotel deserves the same restraint. Guests should not have to live in our mess.

### Discretion

Private clients are not named, on this site or in passing. We publish the commissions that are already public, and we leave the rest off the page.

### Our word

We prefer a straightforward conversation and a clear agreement. We commit to the commissions we take on. An agreed specification and an agreed time are the job.

How we work

## The people who start are the people who finish.

Mr Wallcover is a highly organised specialist team, and the same people see a job through from the first day to the final handover.

- A core team of six wallpaper hangers and six decorators who work together every day, scaling to 25–35 skilled tradespeople for large hotel programmes.
- Risk assessments and method statements (RAMS) on every job, with Dorin Burcus supervising on site.
- Nights and weekends when the building needs it. We have finished hotel floors while guests stayed on the other floors, and worked night shifts in an operational Belgravia restaurant so it could trade undisturbed by day.
- Clean, protected and discreet: dust-screen systems, floor protection, and respect for occupied homes and live hotels.
- 3D scanning of spaces, so drops, pattern repeats and setting-out are resolved before a roll is opened.
- Registered for VAT and CIS, with experience of JCT contracts, working alongside quantity surveyors, contracts managers and design teams.
- Surveying, sourcing, management, installation and aftercare: one accountable team for the whole wallcovering package.

A short timeline

## From 2012 to now.

**2012**
: Dorin Burcus enters the trade on London sites, starting as a labourer.

**2016**
: Four Seasons Hotel London at Ten Trinity Square begins, running to 2019. Novotel London Canary Wharf.

**2017**
: Our Instagram record, @mrwallcover, begins in October. First work at DoubleTree by Hilton London – West End, and at DoubleTree Hyde Park.

**2018**
: A guest-room programme of more than 15,000 m² at the Millennium Hotel, Grosvenor Square, now The Biltmore Mayfair. Huntsman, Savile Row begins. Oxygen Freejumping at The O2; the Everyman cinema, Liverpool; an office on Jermyn Street.

**2019**
: About 5,000 m² completed at Four Seasons Ten Trinity Square. Huntsman completed. DoubleTree West End: 30 rooms on two floors while the hotel stayed open. Trematon Castle begins. Masterpiece London; the House of Hackney Shoreditch showroom; a Mercedes-AMG showroom; Hilton London Metropole; a Little Venice home for Jaillon Studio.

**2020**
: Pendley Manor: 30 rooms in 30 days. The Marylebone; the Adam Reed salon. Jumeirah Carlton Tower: 8 months, finished at the end of 2020.

**2021**
: Chosen to install about 17,500 m² of wallcoverings in 18 months at The OWO. The Westminster London, Curio Collection by Hilton; 250 City Road; the tea house for Jaillon Studio.

**2022**
: Brown's Hotel, Mayfair, begins. Hilton Garden Inn Silverstone. The House of Hackney flagship at St Michael's Clergy House opens.

**2023**
: DoubleTree by Hilton London – Victoria.

**2024–2026**
: Exterior works in Pimlico; Calico event installations.

Wallpapering / preparation

# Wallpaper preparation tables

Use these tables to look up a wall's substrate and condition. They record Mr Wallcover's house practice where the wallpaper maker is silent; the product's current instructions and the actual wall take precedence.

For the reasons behind the checks, read our guide to [assessing whether a wall is ready for wallpaper](https://www.mrwallcover.com/advice/wall-preparation/).

## The rules behind the tables

- **The maker's instruction comes first**

When the wallpaper maker names a primer, a lining grade or a paste for the product, we use what it names.

- **Where the maker is silent, our house standard**

Walls are primed with Zinsser Gardz. Where lining is needed, it is Erfurt lining paper, hung with Wallrock Power Adhesive.

- **We hang only with pastes we have tested**

We hang with the paste the wallpaper maker names, or one of the pastes listed here, which we have tested on our own jobs. Some other pastes we have tried let joints open and the top and bottom edges lift as the paper dried. It is easy to put right, but a client should never have to see it.

- **Our house practice: the lining paste is at least one step above**

We use our own four-step scale to choose a lining paste at least one step above the wallpaper paste. This is Mr Wallcover's house practice, not a cross-brand manufacturer strength standard. When either paste is unknown or the wallpaper paste is at the top step, we check the combination before anything is ordered. The wallpaper maker's instruction takes precedence, including a requirement to use the same paste for both layers.

- **Every lining roll is the same product and grade**

Lining paper has no batch to match, but every roll on a job must be the same product and grade, so the same thickness and the same shade of white.

The products, grades and paste ladder are listed with their data sheets on [lining papers, pastes and primers](https://www.mrwallcover.com/wallpapering/lining-and-adhesives/). The long read, with what each maker says, is [Is the wall ready for wallpaper?](https://www.mrwallcover.com/advice/wall-preparation/)

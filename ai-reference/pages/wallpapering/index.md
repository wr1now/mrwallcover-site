## Fire ratings

A class such as B-s1,d0 applies to the product in the documented build-up, within its permitted variations. Check the exact wallcovering, substrate and adhesive together. The separate reference explains the classes, the four UK nations and the evidence available for named products.

[Fire ratings & standards](https://www.mrwallcover.com/advice/wallcovering-fire-ratings-uk/)

Read the documents as one build-up

1. 01**Substrate**

The wall or board, including its class, thickness and density where specified.

2. 02**Fixing & preparation**

The adhesive and any primer, lining, joints or air gap covered by the evidence.

3. 03**Exact wallcovering**

The product reference, backing, weight, finish and printed variant.

Schematic only. A product classification covers the documented build-up and its permitted variations. These are things to check, not a recommended construction; do not assume an unmentioned layer is included.

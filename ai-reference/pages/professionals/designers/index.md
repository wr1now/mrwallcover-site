### Who can install hand-painted scenic and chinoiserie wallpaper in London?

Mr Wallcover installs hand-painted, scenic and chinoiserie wallpapers in London houses and hotels, in the manner of de Gournay and Fromental. It hangs them. It does not make them, and it is not affiliated with those makers. The scene is set out from a fixed point before the first panel goes up. Numbered panels are hung in the supplied sequence, because on a continuous wall the sequence is the artwork. The delivered panels are checked dry against the maker's layout before any paste. Mr Wallcover measures on site and gives the supplier the finished dimensions of the wall that will actually be hung, before production. The scenic and chinoiserie work on the published record is at Brown's Hotel, Mayfair (2022–2024), including Lewis & Wood's Adam's Eden. Mr Wallcover has installed de Gournay and Fromental papers, including a large volume of Fromental at Four Seasons Ten Trinity Square, as well as House of Hackney, Muraspec and Phillip Jeffries.

### What lead time do you need, and how far ahead should we book?

A start date is given after the survey. If the wallcovering is free-issue, the supplier's lead time sits inside the programme, and the hang is planned around the delivery. For a scenic paper or mural, bring the installer in before the dimensions go to production, because the panels are made to the finished walls. On a tender, the tender deadline is not the requested start date: both should be stated. Lead times depend on the project. When a client or contractor needs support quickly, the team can sometimes mobilise within two to three days.

### Who is responsible if free-issue material is faulty, short or damaged?

Free-issue deliveries are checked against the measure when they arrive. Scenic panels are checked dry against the layout before any paste and before anything is cut, so a sequence, sizing or print problem comes to light while it is still easy to resolve. Quantities are ordered to cover the repeat and the wastage, typically 15–30% and around 30% on staircases, so that the last wall doesn't wait. The specification should say who orders, who receives and checks the delivery, and who approves the batch. The twelve-month guarantee covers the workmanship of the hang: seams opening, edges lifting and adhesive failure that comes from the way the material was hung. Rolls are inspected as part of preparation, although on large volumes not every roll can be opened in advance. Any defect is reported straight away to the designer, the contractor and the manufacturer. If a fault comes from the installation, Mr Wallcover covers the cost of the material.

### Are you on any maker's recommended-installer list?

A maker's name on mrwallcover.com means Mr Wallcover installed that maker's product on that project. It is not a maker approval, accreditation or endorsement. Makers on the published record include Phillip Jeffries (Tweed, at Four Seasons Ten Trinity Square, and at The Biltmore Mayfair, 2018), Omexco, Vescom, Lewis & Wood, Timorous Beasties, House of Hackney, Lincrusta and Calico Wallpaper. Mr Wallcover is not on maker recommended-installer lists. Major main contractors recommend its work. ### How do you price, and what does it cost?

There is no flat rate per roll. The quotation comes from a site survey or from drawings. It reflects the measured area after openings are taken off, the pattern repeat and the wastage it creates (typically 15–30%, with staircases around 30%), the state of the walls, the material, and access and protection. Access, protection and making good are written into the scope rather than discovered at the end, and the quotation says what has been allowed for. The site publishes no prices.

### Will you keep our project confidential, and will you sign an NDA?

Private clients are not named, on the site or in passing. Residential work is published only by street or area, and only with the homeowner's agreement. Private and NDA jobs are anonymised. On the published record, for example, the Heathrow client appears as "a luxury fashion house (name withheld)", and the arch case study's location as "a prime residence in central London", with window views and personal items blurred. Permission for photography is agreed before any image is used. Where a client requires a non-disclosure agreement, Mr Wallcover signs one, and those projects are never named.

### Do you take on single rooms, or only larger projects?

We focus on whole residences and hotel packages, and will take on one- or two-room commissions. The minimum installation commission is one room.

### Do you offer limewash and lime render?

Limewash is applied by Mr Wallcover's own team. Lime render can be arranged through trusted specialist partners.

### Is the joinery made by your own team?

Mr Wallcover is a wallcovering specialist, not a joinery company. We create and finish panels for joinery. For full joinery services, we can work with trusted, vetted specialist partners, coordinating the wallcovering details, installation and aftercare with their work.

### Do you install acoustic wallcoverings and panels?

Acoustic wallcovering and acoustic panel installation in London hotels, offices and homes. Hung to the maker's instruction so the face keeps its performance.

### What aftercare and guarantee are included?

Aftercare is part of every commission: a return visit about four to six weeks after completion, a priority repair call-out and a twelve-month workmanship guarantee.

### How long have you been in the trade?

Dorin Burcus, founder of Mr Wallcover, has worked in the trade since 2012. Award-winning (2021): Most Outstanding for Wallcovering Installation, BUILD Magazine 2021 Design & Build Awards.

### Have you worked in listed buildings?

Listed buildings on the published record: Hyde London City (Grade II, 1874); Four Seasons Ten Trinity Square (Grade II*); a Grade II-listed Georgian house in North London. We follow the specification and whatever the consents for the building allow.

THE PRACTICE

## A beautiful wall begins long before *the first drop.*

From the first survey to the last carefully matched seam, we bring care to every stage. Silk, grasscloth, hand-painted paper and bespoke murals. Wallcoverings, hung properly. By people who understand the material.

Mr Wallcover is a London specialist wallcovering installer founded by Dorin Burcus, in the trade since 2012: survey to aftercare for hotels, homes and makers.

The people on site

## One team, throughout.

- Risk assessments and method statements (RAMS) on every job, with Dorin Burcus supervising on site.
- A core team of six wallpaper hangers and six decorators who work together every day, scaling to 25–35 skilled tradespeople for large hotel programmes.
- Nights and weekends when the building needs it. We have finished hotel floors while guests stayed on the other floors, and worked night shifts in an operational Belgravia restaurant so it could trade undisturbed by day.

THE DETAIL IS THE DIFFERENCE

## The room is the vision. *The finish is our craft.*

Setting out around a doorway. Carrying a pattern through a corner. Preparing a wall so raking light reveals the texture, never the substrate. This is the work that makes the whole room feel right.

**Material knowledge**
: Silk, grasscloth, vinyl, textiles, murals and hand-painted papers.

**Care from beginning to end**
: Survey, management, supply, installation and aftercare.

**A considered return**
: A follow-up visit after four to six weeks, with twelve months’ cover on the workmanship of the hang.

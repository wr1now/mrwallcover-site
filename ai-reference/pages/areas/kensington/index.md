# Wallpaper installation in Kensington

Townhouses, mansion flats and family houses in Kensington and Holland Park. Surveying, supply, installation and aftercare from one London practice.

[Request a quotation](https://www.mrwallcover.com/contact/) [See the work](https://www.mrwallcover.com/projects/)

Kensington houses range from Victorian stucco to later mansion blocks, and the walls vary as much as the architecture. The survey is a judgement on the wall as well as a measure of it. If the wall is not ready for the material specified, we say so before the last drop is ordered.

We hang silk, grasscloth and other natural weaves, fabric walling, vinyl, hand-painted papers in the manner of de Gournay and Fromental, murals and acoustic wallcoverings. Paper can be hung to joinery where the drawings show it.

Private clients are not named. Residential work appears only by street or area, with the owner's agreement. The public record of our work is on the projects page.

### Which wallcoverings do you hang in Kensington homes?

Silk, grasscloth and natural weaves, fabric walling, vinyl and contract wallcoverings, hand-painted papers, murals, and acoustic wallcoverings and panels.

### How much extra wallpaper should I order?

Enough to cover the pattern repeat and the wastage it creates. That allowance is typically 15–30%, depending on the pattern repeat and the layout, with staircases at the high end (around 30%) because of the angles. Extra rolls for pattern matching are confirmed at survey.

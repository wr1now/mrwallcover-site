# Wallpaper installation in Belgravia

Stucco townhouses, mansion flats and hotels in Belgravia and Knightsbridge, SW1. We survey, supply where asked, hang, and return once the paper has settled.

Belgravia houses often have tall rooms, deep cornices and original joinery. That means long drops, careful set-out from a fixed point, and protection of the plasterwork and woodwork that is already there.

### Can you hang wallpaper in a listed Belgravia townhouse?

Yes. We follow the specification and whatever the consents for the building allow. If a wall is not ready for the material, we say so at the survey, before the paper is ordered.

### Do you work with interior designers in Belgravia?

Yes. Paper is usually free-issue from the designer or the client. We manage delivery, check the quantities against our measure, and hang to the designer's specification.

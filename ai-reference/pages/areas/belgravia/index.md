### Can you hang wallpaper in a listed Belgravia townhouse?

Yes. We follow the specification and whatever the consents for the building allow. If a wall is not ready for the material, we say so at the survey, before the paper is ordered.

### Do you work with interior designers in Belgravia?

Yes. The designer or the client usually supplies the paper (free-issue). We manage delivery, check the quantities against our measure, and hang to the designer's specification.

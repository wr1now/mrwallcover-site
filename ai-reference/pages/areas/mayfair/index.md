# Wallpaper installation in Mayfair

Hotels, townhouses and apartments in Mayfair, W1. Our longest recent commission in the area was Brown's Hotel on Albemarle Street, where Mr Wallcover was the main decorating contractor from 2022 to 2024.

[Start your project](https://www.mrwallcover.com/contact/) [See the work](https://www.mrwallcover.com/projects/)

Mayfair rooms tend to carry expensive materials: hand-painted scenic papers, silk, grasscloth and fabric walling. They are also rooms that stay in use while the work goes on, whether that is a hotel floor with guests or a house with a household.

At Brown's Hotel the package was wallcoverings, fine painting, French polishing and other specialist finishes, carried out room by room around a working hotel. The same approach applies to a single drawing room: a contained work area, protection of floors and joinery, and a tidy room at the end of each day.

We survey on site, write the scope room by room, and quantify the paper with the repeat and the wastage it needs. If the paper is supplied by your designer, we receive it, check it against the measure, and hang what was specified.

Brown's is one of three published Mayfair jobs. On Grosvenor Square, at the hotel that is now [The Biltmore Mayfair](https://www.mrwallcover.com/projects/biltmore-mayfair/) (then the Millennium Hotel), we installed a guest-room wallcovering programme of more than 15,000 m² in 2018, on a schedule that included Phillip Jeffries. On Savile Row, from April 2018 to March 2019, we installed [Huntsman](https://www.mrwallcover.com/projects/huntsman-savile-row/)'s bespoke printed wallpaper, a print of the Huntsman tweed, and covered the display boards in the same print.

### Do you work in occupied houses and hotels in Mayfair?

Yes. Most of our Mayfair work has been in rooms that stay in use. At Brown's Hotel the decorating package ran from 2022 to 2024 around a working hotel. Work areas are contained and left tidy each day.

### How do you price wallpaper hanging in Mayfair?

From a site survey or from drawings. The figure follows the measured area after openings are taken off, the pattern repeat, the state of the walls and the material. There is no flat rate per roll.

# Fromental

Fromental creates hand-painted, embroidered and printed wallcoverings. Bespoke panels can be tailored to a room; its separate Shop offers standard formats and colourways. Sample arrangements differ between bespoke and Shop collections.

## Official sourcing routes

Check the route’s scope before ordering. Showrooms, samples and finder links do not establish a roll checkout or delivery promise.

### Showroom or brand contact

Enquire through London showroom or regional contact about bespoke wallcovering; arrange head-office visit by appointment.

**Region**
: UK / Europe; regional overseas contacts

**UK access**
: Explicit London showroom and UK/Europe head office.

**Overseas access**
: Regional contacts listed; FAQ expressly offers international shipping with fees depending on location.

Maker-published information; confirm current eligibility and destination before ordering.

Terms and checks before ordering

Bespoke sample loans require an approved trade account and return within the allocated period. Ask the sales representative for the applicable return date. Head-office visits are by appointment; separate London showroom details appear on the official Visit us page.

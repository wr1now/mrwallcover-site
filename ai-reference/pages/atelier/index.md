## How the preview is made

Your photo is loaded in the browser only; it is not sent to us or to anyone. If you paste a link to a product page and our link reader is switched on, that address alone is sent to it so it can fetch the page. The four corners you place on the wall tell the preview where the wall's rectangle sits in the picture, and the width and height you give turn that rectangle into real centimetres. The pattern is then laid across it drop by drop, at the roll width and repeat you set, with a half-drop staggered on alternate drops, and the light and shade of your own photograph are kept on top. Nothing is uploaded, no account is made and no cookie is set.

### What it can show

- How big a repeat really is on your wall, and how many drops run across it.
- Where the pattern falls at a corner, a dado or a door case, and what moving it by a few centimetres does.
- A large-scale design against a small one, in the same room and the same light.

### What it cannot show

- Colour. Screens, phone cameras and printed paper all differ; a sample in the room, in daylight and at night, is the only colour check.
- Texture and sheen. Grasscloth, silk, vinyl and flock all catch light in ways a flat image does not.
- The set-out we would choose. Where the first drop goes, how the pattern is centred and how corners are turned are decisions made on site; see our guide to [set-out and pattern matching](https://www.mrwallcover.com/advice/set-out-and-pattern-matching/).
- Anything in front of the wall. Furniture, radiators and pictures inside the four corners are papered over unless “Find the wall” has cut them out; otherwise crop the photo or move the corners.

### Why we built it this way

Room visualisers offered by makers apply their own catalogue to a photo; the small print of one widely used visualiser says the scale may vary on your own picture. We are installers: the questions we get are about scale, repeat and where the pattern lands, so this preview asks for one real measurement and is honest about the rest. For a measured room with doors, windows and several walls, [the calculator](https://www.mrwallcover.com/calculator/) gives the quantity and cutting plan, and [a conversation](https://www.mrwallcover.com/contact/?intent=install) gives the set-out.

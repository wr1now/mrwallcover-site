## The maker's instruction comes first

The maker's instruction for the product comes first. Where the maker is silent, we follow our house standard: Erfurt lining paper, hung with a paste at least one step stronger than the wallpaper's. We say which is which: the [lining and adhesives page](/wallpapering/lining-and-adhesives/) shows the maker's instruction and our standard side by side.

## Every fact has a source and a date

- We copy a product fact as the maker prints it, and link the document it comes from: a data sheet, hanging instructions or the product page.
- Next to it we give the date we checked that document. Makers change their documents, so the date tells you how current our copy is.
- Where a page is silent, we write "not stated". We do not fill the gap from another product, another colourway or the brand as a whole.

## Checked twice

Facts are checked once when they are gathered, and again by a person before they appear on this site. Anything that has not had its second check stays off the site.

## Words we do not use without a document

We do not call a product fire-rated, washable or waterproof unless the maker's own document says so for that exact product, and then we use the maker's words. We do not call a seller authorised, approved or exclusive unless the maker's own page says so.

## Money

- No maker or seller pays to appear on this site, or to be listed higher.
- There are no affiliate links. If we ever add one, it will be labelled as an advert, marked as sponsored, and it will not change anything we recommend.
- Where makers or sellers are listed, the order never depends on payment or commission.
- We do not show wallpaper prices, because they change. Where a price matters, we link to the seller's site. The [calculator](/calculator/) gives no installation price.

## Corrections

If something here is wrong or out of date, tell us which page and what the maker's document says. A corrected fact shows the date it was checked again.

## Makers we have installed

**de Gournay.** Hand-painted scenic and panelled papers. They need a sound wall, a patient set-out and time. See [hand-painted wallpaper installation](/services/hand-painted-wallpaper-installation/).

**Fromental.** Scenic and panelled papers, including a large volume at [Four Seasons Hotel London at Ten Trinity Square](/projects/four-seasons-ten-trinity-square/).

**House of Hackney.** Hung across the Georgian house at the [Castle of Trematon](/projects/trematon-castle/) (PLANTASIA, GAIA, FLORA DELANICA, COSMOS, FLORA FANTASIA, ARTEMIS and HOLLYHOCKS), in the maker's own [showroom at St Michael's Clergy House](/projects/house-of-hackney-st-michaels/), in the suite at [Hyde London City](/projects/old-bailey-hotel/) (LIMERENCE) and in a [listed Georgian house in North London](/projects/north-london-residence/) (BAMBUSA).

**Muraspec.** Contract wallcoverings. See [hotel wallcovering installation](/services/hotel-wallcovering-installation/).

**Phillip Jeffries.** Tweed, a tailored textile wallcovering, in the guest rooms and suites of [Four Seasons Hotel London at Ten Trinity Square](/projects/four-seasons-ten-trinity-square/).

## Also on our published case studies

**Timorous Beasties.** Tropical Clouded Leopard and Totem Damask, custom-printed as vinyl, through the guest rooms of [Hyde London City](/projects/old-bailey-hotel/).

**Vescom.** A bespoke wallcovering made for the hotel, and Vescom Alcantara in selected suites, at [Raffles London at The OWO](/projects/raffles-london-the-owo/).

**Omexco.** Belgian wallcoverings, at [Four Seasons Hotel London at Ten Trinity Square](/projects/four-seasons-ten-trinity-square/).

**Lewis & Wood.** Adam's Eden, designed by Adam Calkin, at [Brown's Hotel, Mayfair](/projects/browns-hotel-mayfair/).

**Lincrusta.** The embossed heritage relief wallcovering, at the [Castle of Trematon](/projects/trematon-castle/).

## Calico Wallpaper

Work delivered in partnership with Calico Wallpaper. The Brooklyn studio's murals are on our record at [Lee Broom's Shoreditch showroom](/projects/calico-lee-broom-overture/), [BEVERLY 1975's Cadence at The Lavery](/projects/calico-beverly-1975-cadence/), [Ahluwalia's Estuary at Rosewood London](/projects/calico-ahluwalia-estuary-rosewood/) and, overnight in a live terminal, [airside at Heathrow Terminal 4](/projects/heathrow-terminal-4-calico/).

## What a specified paper needs from us

The maker's hanging instructions come first. Panels are hung in their numbered sequence, batches are checked from the roll labels, and the set-out is agreed before the first length is cut. The [materials library](/materials/) describes each family, and the guide to [hand-painted papers and murals](/advice/hand-painted-murals-set-out/) explains the set-out.

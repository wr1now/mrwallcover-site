## On the public record

**Castle of Trematon, Cornwall (2019–2022).** A Norman motte-and-bailey of 1068, granted to the Duchy of Cornwall in 1337, with an 11,000 sq ft Georgian manor added within the walls in the early 1800s. We hung House of Hackney wallpapers across the Georgian house, including large-scale murals and papered ceilings, in a house where every wall is a little out of true and every ceiling a little out of level. In November 2021 the team installed Lincrusta, the embossed heritage wallcovering made since 1877, hung to a laser line. [The Trematon case study](/projects/trematon-castle/).

**Raffles London at The OWO, Whitehall (2020–2023).** The Old War Office, designed by William Young and completed in 1906 in the Edwardian Baroque. We installed the hotel's wallcoverings in the bedrooms and several other areas: a bespoke Vescom wallcovering made for the hotel, and Vescom Alcantara in some suites. Every wall was prepared and primed to a true, even surface, and the work was sequenced alongside the other trades and the main contractor within one of London's most complex heritage restorations. [The OWO case study](/projects/raffles-london-the-owo/).

**Hyde London City, 15 Old Bailey (2022–2024).** A Grade II-listed hotel completed in 1874 as the Spiers & Pond hotel. The complete decorating package for 111 guest rooms across seven storeys, with specialist wallcoverings and window film. Carved stone bays, oculus windows and a timber-railed staircase all had to be decorated around, protected and finished to a standard that suits both a listed building and a design-led hotel. [The Hyde London City case study](/projects/old-bailey-hotel/).

**Four Seasons Hotel London at Ten Trinity Square (2016–2019).** Guest room and suite wallpapering inside the Grade II*-listed building on Tower Hill. [The Four Seasons case study](/projects/four-seasons-ten-trinity-square/).

**A Grade II-listed Georgian house, North London (2019).** House of Hackney's BAMBUSA in a guest bedroom with a built-in writing desk, exposed beams and floorboards that slope with age. The owner is a private client and is not named. [The North London case study](/projects/north-london-residence/).

## Old walls, prepared for the paper

The survey covers the state of the walls as well as the measure, so the preparation is in the scope from the start. In a historic house that can mean old lime plaster, walls out of plumb, tall stair halls and rooms that were never square. Our guide to [wall preparation](/advice/wall-preparation/) sets out how the substrate, lining and primer are decided, and where the maker's instructions and the specifier take precedence.

## Limewash and lime render

Limewash is applied by Mr Wallcover's own team. Lime render is carried out with trusted specialist partners.

## Consents and the specification

We follow the specification and whatever the consents for the building allow. The specifier and the consents decide what may be done to historic fabric; we plan the setting-out, preparation and protection around those decisions, and around the building as it is.

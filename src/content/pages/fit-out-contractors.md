## The paperwork the package needs

Risk assessments and method statements (RAMS) are issued for every package. Mr Wallcover is registered for VAT and CIS, and has experience of JCT contracts as a specialist package contractor. Public liability, employers' liability and professional indemnity details, and the team's site cards, are available on request.

## One person responsible on site

Dorin Burcus personally supervises the site. The core team is six wallpaper hangers and six decorators, and it scales to 25–35 people on large projects. At Hyde London City, 25 people were on site across preparation, filling, sanding, painting and hanging, from a finished sample room in November 2022 to final touches in August 2024.

## Around the programme, not against it

Night shifts, out-of-hours and weekend work are part of the job, including in live hotels. At Heathrow Terminal 4 a Calico Wallpaper mural was hung overnight in a live terminal. Lead times depend on the project, and the team can sometimes mobilise within 2–3 days to support a client or contractor.

## Free-issue material

Rolls are inspected as part of preparation; on large volumes not every roll can be opened in advance. Any defect is reported straight away to the designer, contractor and manufacturer. If a fault comes from the installation, Mr Wallcover covers the cost of the material.

## Agree the handover

Set out any sample-area approval, snagging and documentation requirements at tender. The [package guide](/advice/developer-wallcovering-package/) lists what to settle before the first room is released.

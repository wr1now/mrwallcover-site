## Our own photographs

Most photographs on this site were taken by Mr Wallcover on our own projects, many of them by Dorin Burcus, the founder. They are © Mr Wallcover. You may not copy, publish, crop or adapt them without our permission.

To ask permission, email [info@mrwallcover.com](mailto:info@mrwallcover.com) with the page address of the photograph and how you would like to use it.

## Photographs by others

Some photographs belong to other people, and each one is credited where it appears. They include the official room photographs of Raffles London at The OWO, House of Hackney's photographs of its St Michael's Clergy House showroom, Hilton's press photographs of Hilton Garden Inn Silverstone, and views of buildings from Wikimedia Commons. Those owners hold the rights, not us, and we cannot license their photographs to anyone. Ask the credited owner.

Wikimedia Commons photographs are used under the Creative Commons licence named in their credit, which sets the terms for reusing them. Where a photographer's name is not recorded, the photograph is still not ours, and we do not claim it.

## Corrections

If a credit is wrong or missing, or you own a photograph shown here and want it changed or removed, email [info@mrwallcover.com](mailto:info@mrwallcover.com) and say which page.

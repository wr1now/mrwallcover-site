No maker or seller pays to appear on this site, and there are no affiliate links. Where we name a seller, it is because the maker's own page lists it, and we give the date we checked.

## Five ways wallpaper is sold

- **The maker's own shop.** The maker sells its own collection from its website or showroom. You can tell because the website belongs to the maker, and every paper on it is the maker's.
- **A retailer.** A shop or website that sells many makers' papers. Delivery, samples and returns follow the retailer's own terms, which can differ from the maker's.
- **A showroom.** A design-centre or showroom that holds a maker's pattern books and takes orders. Samples and orders often go through an account or a designer.
- **Trade only.** The maker sells only to designers and the trade, through an account. A homeowner buys through their designer, or asks us to help.
- **Made to order.** Murals, hand-painted panels and bespoke colourways are made to your measurements. You order by enquiry, and the maker gives the lead time when you do.

## How to tell a genuine stockist

The maker's own "where to buy" or stockist page is the proof that it recognises a seller. We call a seller authorised only when that page lists it, and we open the page before we say so. A seller that is not listed may still sell the paper honestly: ask where the rolls come from, and whether they will all come from one batch.

## Order a sample first

- Look at the sample on the wall it is meant for, in daylight and again in the evening.
- Check that the sample's product code is the code you will order.
- Keep it. When the rolls arrive, compare them with the sample before anything is cut.

Sample terms differ from seller to seller: some are free, some are charged, and some are for trade accounts only. The seller's own page has the current terms.

## Order every roll at once

Order every roll you need, plus the spare, at the same time, and ask for a single batch. Before the first drop is cut, check that every roll has the same batch number. To count the rolls, use the [calculator](/calculator/): it gives the working rolls, the spare on its own line, and the cutting plan.

## Lead times and returns

Ask the seller for the delivery date before you book the hanging. Papers in stock and papers printed or made to order can take very different times to arrive.

Read the returns terms before you order a mural or a made-to-measure paper. The legal right to cancel an order made online or by phone does not cover goods made to the buyer's specifications or clearly personalised ([Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013, regulation 28(1)(b)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2013/3134/regulation/28), checked 17 October 2026).

## Buying from abroad

Paper sent to Great Britain from outside the UK can carry VAT and Customs Duty. Depending on the value of the order, you may pay the VAT to the delivery company before the parcel is handed over, and it sends a bill for any VAT, duty and delivery charges. Northern Ireland has different rules ([GOV.UK, Tax and customs for goods sent from abroad](https://www.gov.uk/goods-sent-from-abroad/tax-and-duty), checked 17 October 2026). Before you order, ask the seller whether the price includes tax and duty.

What is a double cut, and where should it be made? A double cut joins two drops by overlapping them and cutting through both at once, so the two edges meet exactly. On patterned, delicate and premium wallcoverings, we make that cut **on the table, not on the wall**, using a method we developed ourselves: the **Mr Wallcover magnet table cut**. And we never double cut on the wall over lining paper.

## Why never double cut on the wall over lining paper?

A double cut made on the wall goes through everything under the blade. Over lining paper, that means the wallcovering **and** the lining. As the adhesive dries, the paper pulls. With the lining cut too, the pull lifts both layers and the seam opens.

That seam is close to impossible to repair. We have saved one only a few times, with great difficulty, so on our sites the rule is absolute. One maker gives the same warning in its own instructions: [Timorous Beasties](https://www.timorousbeasties.com/mediaLibrary/other/english/129396.pdf) advises against cross-lining where seams will be double cut, because the knife can slice the lining and the seams can then split.

## How does the magnet table cut work?

1. **Two drops, one on top of the other.** Both drops go on the cutting table, one laid over the other. Many installers cut one drop at a time; cutting both together is what makes the edges a perfect pair.
2. **The pattern matched exactly.** The drops are overlapped where the design lines up, by the overlap the maker recommends. The design repeat and the pattern match decide it.
3. **Clamped between two straight edges.** One metal straight edge goes under both drops, and a second goes on top, directly over it. Six to eight strong magnets lock the two together through the paper, so neither the drops nor the guide can creep. Our straight edges are 3 m and 4 m long, so a full drop is cut in one pass with no join in the guide.
4. **One cut, onto metal.** The blade runs down the top straight edge and cuts onto the metal straight edge underneath. A fresh segment of a snap-off blade is used for every cut.
5. **Butted on the wall.** The two edges came from the same cut, so they meet exactly when the drops are hung side by side.

Because the cut happens on the table, no blade touches the wall: no scored plaster, and no cut lining. We developed this method ourselves, and other installers have since adopted it. Our [Instagram post of 31 October 2021](https://www.instagram.com/p/CVsCJXzqeHD/) shows the straight edge clamped over a textile on the cutting table.

## Which wallcoverings get a table cut?

Patterned, delicate, high-end and premium wallcoverings: fabrics, patterned fabrics, silks and similar. **We have never table cut vinyl.** Wide contract vinyl is joined on the wall by [overlap and splice](/advice/set-out-and-pattern-matching/#what-is-overlap-and-splice), and most contract vinyls are hung without a liner.

Some products are never double cut at all. [de Gournay](https://degournay.com/uploads/technical/document/2026_Hanging_Instructions-After-2025.pdf)'s hand-painted panels, for example, are butted, not lapped and cut on the wall. The maker's instructions for the exact product always come first.

## Does lining paper have to be cross-lined?

No. Cross-lining (horizontal lining) is one option, but lining can also be hung **vertically**, as long as every wallpaper seam sits **20 to 50 mm away** from the lining joints. The point is the same either way: the joints of the lining and the joints of the finish never meet. The [wall preparation guide](/advice/wall-preparation/#what-is-lining-paper-for-and-does-this-wall-need-it) explains what lining is for and what makers ask for.

## Which papers grow when they are pasted?

**Traditional (woven) paper lining** grows when the adhesive goes on. We call it woven because its texture is very like a woven fabric. **Traditional paper-based wallpapers** grow too, including the many designs printed or hand-painted onto a paper base. [Anaglypta](https://www.anaglypta.co.uk/faqs/) puts the growth at up to 10 mm for lining, against 3 to 6 mm for its traditional papers. [A.S. Création](https://www.as-creation.com/en/advice-help/wallpaper-advice/wallpapering-tips/paper-wallpaper-hanging-instructions) gives a 53 cm paper growing to about 54 cm.

**Non-woven** lining and non-woven wallpapers stay stable: they do not expand when pasted. This is why the soaking time on a traditional paper's label matters. The paper should do its growing before it reaches the wall, not on it.

## When does this guide stop being reliable?

It describes our own method and the reasons for it. The hanging instructions for the exact product always decide the joint, the overlap and whether it may be cut at all. For a specialist wallcovering, send the product name, its hanging instructions if you have them, and photographs of the walls.

Jumeirah Carlton Tower stands on Cadogan Place in Knightsbridge, above Sloane Street and the private Cadogan Gardens. We worked on the hotel's renovation for eight months in 2020, installing wallcoverings, and finished at the end of 2020. The hotel reopened in 2021.

## At a glance

- **Hotel:** Jumeirah Carlton Tower, Cadogan Place, Knightsbridge, London
- **When:** 2020, eight months, finished at the end of 2020
- **Role:** wallcovering installation during the hotel's renovation

## The rooms

Jumeirah describes the renovated bedrooms and suites as light oak wall panelling with textured wallcoverings set into the panels, which soften the rooms and give them a more residential feel. Inset work of this kind leaves nowhere to hide: each panel is its own small wall, and every edge meets a moulding or a frame, so the cuts have to be clean and the texture has to run the same way in every panel of a room.

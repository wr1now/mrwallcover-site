In September 2019 we supplied and fitted black matt Muraspec wallcoverings at one of the newest Mercedes showrooms in London, the AMG showroom photographed here. Black matt is the least forgiving finish there is: it shows every joint, every mark and every uneven wall under showroom lighting.

## At a glance

- **Where:** a Mercedes-AMG showroom in London
- **When:** September 2019
- **Scope:** supply and installation of black matt wallcoverings
- **Wallcovering:** Muraspec, black matt
- **Detail:** brushed stainless-steel trims around the doors and above the skirtings

## Black matt

On a dark matt finish the joints are invisible only if the walls beneath are flat and the edges are cut clean, so the preparation decides the result. Hands, paste and tools are kept off the face of the material, because a mark on black matt cannot be wiped away.

## The trims

Around each door and above each skirting a narrow brushed stainless-steel trim finishes the edge of the wallcovering. The wallcovering is cut to meet the trim on one continuous line, so the steel reads as a deliberate frame rather than a cover strip.

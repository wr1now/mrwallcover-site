In January 2018 we installed a Wall&decò WET System inside the shower of a private home in London, with supply through West One Bathrooms, and worked on other areas of the same room. The home is not named.

## At a glance

- **Where:** a private residence, London
- **When:** January 2018
- **Scope:** a waterproof wet system inside the shower, and other areas of the room
- **Wallcovering:** Wall&decò WET System, the generation before AQUABOUT

## A mural inside the shower

A lotus in soft greens and teals runs straight into the shower, where ordinary wallpaper cannot go. Wall&decò's WET System is not a paper but a sealed build-up made on the wall: a water-tanking primer, a dedicated adhesive, a printed fibreglass fabric carrying the design, and a clear protective finish. The design is printed to the size of the wall, and the drops are butted so the mural runs on without a break.

## The niche

A long recessed niche, lined in small mosaic tiles, sits inside the mural. The fabric is fitted around it and the niche edge is closed with a metal trim, as every niche on our wet-system jobs is.

## How we work on every wet system

We install a wet system only on new plaster, new boarding or new walls, never over existing tiles. Every sealant joint, at the tray, the screen and the tile edges, is made by a certified sealant specialist. The shower can be used after about two days, and the system keeps curing for seven to fourteen days. The [bathrooms and wet rooms guide](/advice/bathrooms-showers-and-wet-rooms/) explains the method in full.

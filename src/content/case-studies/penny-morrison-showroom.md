## At a glance

- **Client:** Penny Morrison, at The Fabric Collective
- **Address:** 9 Langton Street, Chelsea, London SW10
- **Internal works:** December 2023 to January 2024
- **Exterior:** April to May 2024
- **Scope:** all the internal works plus the full exterior

## Inside

The showroom and the stair hall were decorated and papered in Penny Morrison's own floral papers. Joinery and electrics were coordinated with the decorating so the rooms could be finished as one interior. Pattern names are not stated here.

## Outside

The full front elevation followed in April and May 2024: windows, stucco and brick, and the entrance. The same commission covers the interior and the exterior.

A tea house in West London, designed by [Jaillon Studio](https://www.jaillonstudio.com/), the Paris interior design studio, and wallpapered by us for its reopening in 2021. The main room is wrapped in a large-scale green-and-gold feather wallpaper, hung around a run of arched mirrors; the garden room is a panoramic scenic paper of a swan lake and flowering hydrangeas.

## At a glance

- **Designer:** [Jaillon Studio](https://www.jaillonstudio.com/), Paris
- **Where:** a tea house in West London
- **Year:** 2021
- **Our role:** wallpaper and scenic mural installation
- **Photographs:** July 2021, the finished feather-wallpaper room first. A reflection and a passer-by outside are blurred.

## The feather room

A pattern this large leaves nowhere to hide. A drop hung a few millimetres off shows as a broken quill at the seam, and the eye travels along the repeat from one end of the room to the other.

**Setting out around the mirrors.** The room is lined with arched mirrors set into the wall. We set out the repeat from the mirrors rather than from a corner, so the pattern sits centred and symmetrical on each one and the cut edges around the frames fall in the same place on the motif every time.

**Matching every drop.** The pattern was matched drop by drop across the full height of the walls, so the feathers run unbroken from the skirting to the ceiling and around the internal corners.

**Clean edges.** The paper was trimmed tight to every mirror frame and the counter, so the dark ground of the pattern meets each fitting on a crisp line.

## The garden room

The garden room is a panoramic scenic wallpaper: a swan lake under willows, with hydrangeas in the foreground. A scenic paper is hung as a sequence of numbered panels that join into one picture, so the order of the panels, the alignment of the horizon and the joins between them decide whether it reads as a painting or as a row of strips. Gilt-framed mirrors set into the scene extend the view.

## The result

A dark, jewel-toned tea room where the walls carry the design: the feathers read as one continuous surface around every mirror, and the swan lake runs as a single picture across the garden room.

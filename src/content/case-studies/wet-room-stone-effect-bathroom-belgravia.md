In January 2020 we installed a grey stone-effect Glamora waterproof wallcovering through the bathroom of a private home in Belgravia, including the shower and a window reveal. The home is not named.

## At a glance

- **Where:** a private residence, Belgravia, London
- **When:** January 2020
- **Scope:** a waterproof wallcovering through the bathroom, including the shower and a window reveal
- **Wallcovering:** Glamora, grey stone effect

## A different kind of wet system

Glamora's waterproof system is a thin, pre-finished sheet, unlike Wall&decò's, which is built up in layers on the wall. Inside a shower cubicle, Glamora asks for a waterproofing coat on the wall first. We applied it before hanging.

## The window in the wet zone

The window sits in the bathroom's wet zone. The wallcovering is wrapped into the reveal and finished cleanly against the marble sill. Elsewhere it runs below a marble capping, above marble skirting.

## Before the sanitaryware

The wallcovering went on before the sanitaryware, while the pipe tails were still out. Sealant at the wet junctions follows our standing rule: a certified sealant specialist makes every joint.

The [bathrooms and wet rooms guide](/advice/bathrooms-showers-and-wet-rooms/) explains how we choose and install wallcoverings in bathrooms.

## The building

Huntsman has traced its roots to 1809, when it began as a gaiter and breeches maker. Henry Huntsman took the business over in 1849, and the house moved to 11 Savile Row in 1919. The building is a Georgian terraced house of about 1733 with a mid-twentieth-century shopfront. The front shop keeps its pair of stag heads, left by a customer in 1921. The cutting room and workshops are on the premises. The shop's appearance in the Kingsman films made its fitting room familiar to a new audience.

Masterpiece London was the summer art and antiques fair in the grounds of the Royal Hospital Chelsea. In June 2019 we installed a bespoke, hand-crafted wallcovering on one of its stands: bold abstract shapes in teal, red and blue, outlined in gold.

## At a glance

- **Where:** Masterpiece London 2019, Royal Hospital Chelsea
- **When:** June 2019
- **Scope:** installation of a bespoke hand-crafted wallcovering on an exhibition stand
- **Set-out:** laser levels

## A fair stand

A fair stand is built and dressed in days, and it is seen close up by every visitor. A hand-crafted design has no repeat to fall back on, so each panel is placed where the design needs it, set out with laser levels so the outlines run through from one panel to the next.

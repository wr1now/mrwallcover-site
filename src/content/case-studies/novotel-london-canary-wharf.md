We installed wallcoverings at Novotel London Canary Wharf in 2016. In January 2022 we went back to enjoy the work of our hands, and these photographs are from that visit: concrete-effect and timber-grain wallcoverings in the rooms and corridors, six years on.

## At a glance

- **Hotel:** Novotel London Canary Wharf
- **When:** 2016 (photographed in January 2022)
- **Scope:** wallcovering installation
- **Finishes seen here:** concrete-effect and timber-grain wallcoverings

## Six years on

A hotel wallcovering is judged years after the installers leave: whether the joints have stayed closed, the corners have held and the edges are still tight to the frames after daily use. These photographs show the rooms in 2022, after six years in service.

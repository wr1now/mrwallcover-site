## The building

Motcombs opened on Motcomb Street in 1982, when Philip Lawless, formerly the manager of Scott's on Mount Street, turned a small café into a restaurant and bar. For more than forty years it was a Belgravia institution, known for its "home away from home" feel, its Dickensian windows and walls hung with an eclectic collection of art. The restaurant had a total refurbishment in 2021. The Belgravia site closed in early 2025, and the Motcombs name has since moved to St John's Wood and Mayfair.

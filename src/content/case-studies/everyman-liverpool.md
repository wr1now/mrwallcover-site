In October to December 2018 we installed bespoke digital wallcoverings at the Everyman cinema in Liverpool: extra-wide, large-format prints of classic film and music imagery, printed by Muraspec and hung as single pictures from floor to ceiling.

## At a glance

- **Client:** Everyman cinema, Liverpool
- **When:** October to December 2018
- **Scope:** installation of bespoke large-format digital prints
- **Wallcovering:** Muraspec digital print, extra-wide
- **Imagery:** black-and-white film stills and colour street scenes

## A picture, not a pattern

A digital mural is one image cut into drops. If a drop is hung out of order, a few millimetres high, or stretched as it is pasted, a face or a line breaks at the joint and the picture is lost. Each drop is hung in sequence and matched across the joint, from the top of the wall down.

## Preparing the walls

A large print shows every bump beneath it, so the walls were prepared and lined before the murals went up, as one of the photographs shows. Scaffold towers and platforms gave a safe working height on the tallest walls.

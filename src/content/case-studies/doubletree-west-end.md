## At a glance

- **Hotel:** DoubleTree by Hilton London – West End
- **Address:** 92 Southampton Row, Holborn
- **Years on site:** main works in 2019
- **Scope:** guest room wallcoverings
- **Formerly:** the Bonnington Hotel

## The work

The commission was the guest rooms. Pattern, batch and the order of rooms were agreed before the first length was cut, so a repeated room could stay consistent down a corridor.

The photographs on this page show the Southampton Row building from the street, before and after it took the DoubleTree name. They show the building, not the finished rooms.

## Next step

For a similar guest-room package, send the room types, the programme and who supplies the material; a survey then decides preparation, quantities and the order of rooms.

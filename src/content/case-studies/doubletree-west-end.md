## At a glance

- **Hotel:** DoubleTree by Hilton London – West End
- **Address:** 92 Southampton Row, Holborn
- **Years on site:** main works in 2019
- **Scope:** guest room wallcoverings; in 2019, levels 5 and 6, 30 rooms and their corridors, while the hotel stayed open on the other floors
- **Formerly:** the Bonnington Hotel

## The work

The commission was the guest rooms. Pattern, batch and the order of rooms were agreed before the first length was cut, so a repeated room could stay consistent down a corridor.

In July 2019 we completed levels 5 and 6: 30 Premium and Deluxe rooms and their corridors. The hardest part was working in a hotel that was still open, with guests on the other floors. We first worked here in December 2017, hanging Tektura wide vinyl.

The first photographs on this page show the Southampton Row building from the street, before and after it took the DoubleTree name. They show the building, not the finished rooms. The rest are our own photographs of the finished rooms and corridors.

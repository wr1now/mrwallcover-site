## At a glance

- **Hotel now:** The Biltmore Mayfair, LXR Hotels & Resorts
- **Hotel then:** Millennium Hotel, Grosvenor Square
- **Address:** 44 Grosvenor Square, Mayfair
- **Years on site:** February to November 2018
- **Scope:** a guest-room wallcovering programme of more than 15,000 m², on a schedule that included Phillip Jeffries

## The work

The papers were hung to the schedule for that building, with the repeat and the set-out taken from the supplied drawings rather than from a generic roll count. Phillip Jeffries is named because it was on that schedule. That is not a claim to be a stockist or a manufacturer-approved installer.

## Photographs on this page

Our own photographs of the guest rooms and corridors in 2018, first posted on our Instagram, [@mrwallcover](https://www.instagram.com/mrwallcover/), on 14 June 2018 ([BkBSc6AHX7U](https://www.instagram.com/p/BkBSc6AHX7U/)), when the programme of more than 15,000 m² had recently started. One shows a room during the works, with the floor still protected.

## The building

Bailiffscourt looks like a medieval manor, but the house dates from the 1930s. Walter Guinness, later Lord Moyne, bought the estate at Climping in 1927. Between about 1928 and 1935 the architect and antiquarian Amyas Phillips built a new house in a fifteenth-century style. He gathered old stone, timber, doors, windows and fireplaces from across the country, and re-erected whole buildings on the site. Only the thirteenth-century chapel, now listed Grade II*, survives from the earlier manor. The house became a hotel in 1948.

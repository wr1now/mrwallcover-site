Two jobs at the Hilton London Metropole in Paddington in the autumn of 2019: a jet-black embossed Muraspec wallcovering in September, and in November a Vescom wallcovering on the wall panels of one of the hotel's large conference rooms.

## At a glance

- **Hotel:** Hilton London Metropole, Paddington
- **When:** September to November 2019
- **Scope:** wallcoverings, including the wall panels of a conference room
- **Wallcoverings:** Muraspec (jet black), Vescom

## Wall panels in a conference room

Panelled walls in a conference room are covered panel by panel. Each panel is wrapped so the wallcovering runs the same way on every one, and the edges meet the neighbouring panels on a clean line, because the room is seen all at once from the doors.

## The building

Bishopstrow House is a late-Georgian country house about a mile east of Warminster. An earlier manor house by the River Wylye burned down in 1817. The present house was built for William Temple on a new site and completed in 1821 by John Pinch the Elder of Bath. It is Grade II-listed and sits in a conservation area. The house became a hotel in the late 1970s. Ian and Christa Taylor bought it in 2021, and since then all 36 bedrooms and suites have been refurbished, each one individually designed.

In September 2018 we installed bespoke digital wallcoverings for Oxygen Freejumping at The O2 in Greenwich: a fine-line geometric design printed by Muraspec on wide vinyl, running along the seating areas and corridors.

## At a glance

- **Where:** Oxygen Freejumping, The O2, Greenwich
- **When:** September 2018
- **Scope:** installation of bespoke digital wallcoverings
- **Wallcovering:** Muraspec digital print on wide vinyl
- **Joint:** overlap and splice

## Overlap and splice

Wide vinyl is hung with the edges overlapped, then cut through both layers at once and the offcuts removed, so the two edges meet exactly. On a design of fine white lines on a dark ground, that is the only way to keep each line continuous across the joint.

## At a glance

- **Hotel:** DoubleTree by Hilton London – Victoria
- **Address:** 2 Bridge Place, Victoria
- **Years on site:** March to September 2023
- **Scope:** guest room wallcoverings

## The work

The rooms were hung while the hotel stayed in use. Access, protection and the order of floors were agreed so a finished room could be handed back without the next room waiting on a guess.

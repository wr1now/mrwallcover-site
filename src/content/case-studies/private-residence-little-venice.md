A private home in Little Venice, designed by [Jaillon Studio](https://www.jaillonstudio.com/), the Paris-London studio of Catherine Jaillon. In July 2019 we hung a vibrant House of Hackney floral wallpaper through the living room, around full-height bookcases. The home is not named.

## At a glance

- **Designer:** [Jaillon Studio](https://www.jaillonstudio.com/) (Catherine Jaillon), Paris and London
- **Where:** a private residence in Little Venice, London
- **When:** July 2019
- **Scope:** wallpaper installation
- **Wallpaper:** House of Hackney

## Around the bookcases

A large floral is set out from the centre of the main wall, so the pattern sits balanced in the room, and cut in tight around the bookcases, shelves and frames so the paper looks as though it was always there.

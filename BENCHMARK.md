Research date: 10 October 2026. Public pages inspected; technology and performance are unknown unless observed. Comparisons are qualitative, not lab speed scores. Supplier brands below are presentation references. Installation guidance must come from the practice’s own documented work or the maker’s product instructions.

## Cycle 1 — project enquiries and evidence

The practice accepts installation, sourcing and advice enquiries. Visitors can describe their project using the existing form, with a short checklist and optional fuller brief. These routes describe the services Mr Wallcover offers.

Implemented: full-width hotel imagery with recorded credits, a distinction between finished installation and venue photographs, and clear enquiry choices. Prices and project claims require evidence from the practice's own records.
Validation: production homepage route/media tests; existing enquiry validation tests; browser checks recorded in QA_REPORT.md.

## Cycle 3 — trustworthy AI discovery and release hardening

| Authoritative comparable | Requirement or limit | Opportunity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Google AI features](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features) | Ordinary crawlable, useful pages; no required special AI file or guaranteed inclusion | Rich semantic HTML with the same claims as structured files |
| [OpenAI crawler documentation](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/bots) | Search, training and user-initiated access are distinct | Preserve existing crawler preferences; avoid claiming every agent is supported |
| [llms.txt proposal](https://llmstxt.org/) | Supplemental discovery convention, not a universal standard | Explicit describedby links and existing Markdown alternatives |
| [Schema.org LocalBusiness](https://schema.org/LocalBusiness) and [Service](https://schema.org/Service) | Structured descriptions of a business and services | Reuse factual schema and source-linked datasets |

Implemented foundation: four evidence-based shortlist reasons, eight project-fit scenarios, seven material decisions, exact five-stage delivery and aftercare terms, sixteen project records with scope/approach/outcome extracts, source and photo credits, and a blank unsent enquiry template. Final refinement: explicit machine discovery and build-time link/anchor/parity checks. Unknown prices, availability, project-specific cover and credentials stay explicitly unconfirmed. No AI ranking claims, fabricated reviews or hidden competing version of the business.
Rejected: an AI-only gated page, cloaking, unsupported rating scores, automatic submissions and changing GPTBot policy without an owner decision.
Validation and commit evidence are recorded in PLANS.md and QA_REPORT.md. Public search inclusion can only be assessed after public release; the Sites edition remains owner-private.

# Original Room Studio illustrations

The six WebPs on this introductory page are original procedural wallpaper and Blender architectural studies created for Mr Wallcover on 19 October 2026. They are not supplier designs, customer photographs or completed installations. The original SVG artwork is retained in [artwork](artwork/). The fuller editable application and renderer remain under review in PR #83.

Willow lattice uses a 640 × 640 mm straight repeat in a 6.2 × 4.6 m room with a chimney breast and three windows. Ochre cadence uses a 530 × 640 mm half-drop strip in a 6.0 × 5.0 m L-shaped room. Indigo arcade uses a 600 × 600 mm complete tile in a 5.6 × 4.6 m angled bay room with four windows. These dimensions describe the technical illustrations, not a surveyed customer site.

Full room views are 1920 × 1080 pixels; card previews are 960 × 540. They are actual local Blender output using the measured geometry and calibrated original artwork. No paid processing, AI, supplier assets or private source records are required for this introductory release.

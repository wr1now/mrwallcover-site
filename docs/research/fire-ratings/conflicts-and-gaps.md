# Conflicts, evidence gaps and questions

Prepared 15 October 2026. Conflicts are recorded, not resolved by choosing the stronger claim. Product entries are generated from `src/data/fire-ratings/products.json`, so they match the page; the regulatory and standards entries are from the agents' notes (`uk-regulations.md`, `standards-and-terminology.md`) and the lead's own reading.

## Regulatory

- **England transition wording.** The 2025 amendments booklet says the old edition continues where work is sufficiently progressed within six months beginning on 2 March 2025; the circular's notices say before 2 September 2025. Read as the same date; the Department does not say so.
- **England cross-reference slips.** Approved Document B Volume 2 paragraph 6.13 cites paragraphs B11 and B13 (now B10 and B12), and paragraph 12.22b cites Section 4 for linings (it is Section 6).
- **Table 6.1 note 1.** The note's marker is printed on the circulation-space row only; whether the BS EN 15102 route was intended for rooms as well is not stated. The page describes it as printed.
- **'Residential accommodation'.** Used in Table 6.1 and paragraph 6.4 but not defined in Appendix A as printed. Whether a hotel bedroom is treated as residential accommodation is a design-team and building-control decision.
- **England against Wales and Northern Ireland.** National reaction-to-fire classes were removed from England's guidance on 2 March 2025; Wales and Northern Ireland still print them. Scotland's handbook excludes decorative wallpaper and paint from the classified surface.
- **Older fire risk assessment guides.** The official guides for occupied premises (2006, reprinted) still use Class 0, 1 and 3, while saying the European equivalent is acceptable.
- **Welsh document dates.** The Welsh page says it was last updated on 19 June 2025, but the Volume 1 PDF was re-uploaded on 5 August 2026 with the same edition label.

## Standards

- **BS EN 15102 edition.** BSI lists the 2019 edition (roll form) as current and the 2007+A1:2011 edition (roll and panel form) as withdrawn, while the GB designated-standards list (26 September 2025), the European Commission's list (9 February 2026) and Approved Document B cite 2007+A1:2011.
- **Approved Document B Appendix F** lists older editions than BSI's current ones for BS EN 13501-2, BS EN 15725 and BS EN 13238.
- **Class E subclasses.** Approved Document B paragraph B7 groups class E with the classes that carry subclasses; Delegated Regulation 2016/364 gives class E a droplet subclass (d2) only. The page follows the Regulation and notes it.
- **Paid texts not read.** BS EN 15102 Annex ZA, its mounting and substrate provisions, and how BS EN 13823 measures smoke were not read; the page describes them only at the level of public metadata and the Regulation.

## Questions to put to each maker


**Muraspec**

- Which declaration applies to a given roll where two declarations overlap (CPR0001 and CPR0004 for woven fabric-backed vinyl; CPR0002 and CPR0003 for paper-backed and Type II non-woven vinyl)?
- Is any classification valid on paper-faced plasterboard, plaster or painted walls, or with the Light or Sealed Surface adhesives in the May 2026 hanging leaflet?
- What covers newer digital bases, and how do print coverage and ink affect the class?
- What evidence supports the class C statement for the acoustic foam vinyl, and is there a declaration?

**Tektura**

- Is there a declaration of performance or classification report for each product, and which laboratory issued it?
- What does 'when adhered to a suitable substrate' mean: which boards, adhesive and primer?
- For Temple and the other products whose sheet says B and page says C, which is current?
- What are the smoke and droplet subclasses for products printed only as Euroclass B?

**Vescom**

- Which report supports Colour Choice+Protect, given that the linked report describes a different vinyl without the Protect layer?
- Has certificate 0493-CPR-W014 or W015 been renewed since its stated validity date of 31 July 2023?
- Do printed or coloured designs fall under the plain white 350 g/m² report?
- Is any classification valid on plasterboard, which several reports exclude?

**Arte International**

- Which adhesive and fibre-cement board were used in the tests behind Moire Sauvage, and does the class hold on plasterboard with Clearpro?
- Should the Moire Sauvage declaration read System 1, and will it be reissued?
- For Collines, Riverbank and Aranya, is the declaration (C-s1,d0) or the current report (D-s1,d0) correct?
- Is a starch glue on a substrate of at least 1,350 kg/m³ the only permitted build-up for the type 01, 03 and 06 reports?

**Newmor**

- Which smoke subclass should be specified for the Premier 400 g/m² non-woven, given the test report's 'probable s1, definite s2'?
- Which adhesive grade applies to the Project paper-backed report, which names Light on one page and Heavy on another?
- Is the 2012 Project report still the current evidence?

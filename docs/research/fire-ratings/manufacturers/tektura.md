# Tektura: fire documentation for commercial wallcoverings

Research agent D. Checked 15 October 2026 (all access dates are 2026-10-15). Only Tektura's own documents and pages were used. Machine-readable records: `D-tektura.json` (16 product records, 49 source records).

## 1. Summary

- **What exists.** Tektura (a London contract wallcovering supplier, registered office 34 Harbour Exchange Square, E14) publishes a one-page "technical specification" for almost every one of its 328 listed designs, on the product page under "Technical and data downloads". About 400 files were downloaded and hashed. **No declaration of performance, no EN 13501-1 classification report, no EN 15102 document, no IMO certificate and no test report is published** on tektura.com, and no sheet prints a report number, laboratory, issue date or revision number. The site has no technical, downloads, compliance or sustainability page and no sitemap. Every fire statement is therefore a manufacturer's technical-sheet or webpage statement, not a classification document.
- **What the sheets say** (automated read of the PDF text layer for 289 standard, non-digital wallcovering sheet files, with about 25 of them checked on the rendered page). 157 print only "Euroclass B", with no smoke or droplet subclass. 110 print "Euroclass B" plus a BS 476 "Class 0 and 1" line, still with no subclasses. 13 print B with s1 and d0, and 7 print B with s2 and d0 (the standard is printed only as "13501", "EN3501" or "EN13501"). 2 woven coverings (Shimmy, Hustle) print class C with s1 and d0. Counts are of sheet files; a few sheets cover two designs.
- **Geometrics (the owner-checked sheet) is confirmed on the rendered page.** Page 2 prints the wallcovering fire line as "Euro classification B when adhered to suitable substrate." with no smoke or droplet subclass and no standard cited, followed by a BS 476 parts 6 and 7 Class 0 and 1 line carrying the same substrate condition. Smoke and droplets are therefore recorded as unknown (null). The 4-page PDF is a combined sheet: the window film (p.3) and the acoustic panel (p.4) have their own, separate fire lines.
- **Documents disagree in places.** Temple, Plaster Piece, Savoy and Stonewalk: sheet says Euroclass B, product page says Euroclass C. Ambient: sheet says B (plus BS 476 Class 0 and 1), product page says C. All five sheets and pages were created or changed in 2026. The newer page is not assumed to be right.
- **Build-up is never stated.** No sheet says what substrate, adhesive, primer or lining the classified sample used. "Adhesive: Generally Tekfix Plus, though depends on substrate" is installation guidance. Only the digital sheets and about ten standard sheets add the conditional wording "when adhered to a suitable substrate", and "suitable" is never defined.

**Tested build-up, field of application, limitations and unresolved questions (apply to every record unless stated).**

- Tested build-up: not stated in any document (substrate, adhesive, primer, lining, air gap all unknown, recorded as null).
- Field of application: not stated. The only conditional wording is "when adhered to a suitable substrate" (Geometrics and the other digital sheets, Tiburon Shagreen and nine other standard sheets), which does not define the substrate.
- Limitations: the rating belongs to the named product only; the sheet is not a DoP or classification report; subclasses printed as plain "Euroclass B" are unknown, never s1 or d0; Euroclass is reaction to fire, not fire resistance.
- Unresolved: test standard, edition and methods; classification report or DoP reference and laboratory; tested substrate, adhesive and lining; whether the class holds with Tekfix VOV or Strippable as well as Tekfix Plus; current status of any sheet whose page disagrees.

- **Dimity Weave and Rivulet** have the same printed weight (350 g/m²) and the same described backing but different smoke subclasses (s1 and s2). They are kept separate. IMO 2010 FTP Parts 2 to 5 is printed as "On request" only; no IMO certificate was found, so no marine approval can be stated.

- **Tekwrite** sheets are 2019 files (PDF metadata). The magnetic version warns that its ferrous content can conduct electricity near exposed wiring (a hazard note, not a fire rating).

## 5. Installation conditions relevant to fire (with page references)

None of the documents links a fire class to an installation condition other than the vague "suitable substrate" wording. What they do say:

- **Standard sheets (page 1, Adhesive row):** generally Tekfix Plus, "though depends on substrate"; Tekfix VOV when installing around right-angled corners (Abaca; most sheets). Hanging row: paste the wall, trim allowance 5-10 cm top and bottom, overlap and double cut through, run a seam roller. No substrate, primer or lining paper is named on these sheets.
- **Geometrics sheet (p.2):** adhesive generally Tekfix Plus, depends on substrate; drops overlapped, double cut through, seam roller; the product should not be reverse hung. Fire lines carry the "suitable substrate" condition.
- **Hanging instructions for digitally printed wallcoverings (6 pp, same file attached to at least 14 digital pages; PDF metadata 2014-12 / 2015-01, so over a decade old):**
  - p.1: Tekfix Plus adhesive or Strippable, depending on the surface; Tekfix Primer if required.
  - p.2: remove all old backing paper; surface sound, smooth, clean, completely dry and of uniform colour; fill cracks; site at 12 °C (55 °F) minimum. Lining paper is not mentioned.
  - p.3, "Surfaces": plasterboard, prime with Tekfix Primer, or for fabric-backed vinyls only use Tekfix Strippable directly onto unprimed plasterboard; porous surfaces sealed with Tekfix Primer; non-porous surfaces (glass, gloss paint) abraded and hung with Tekfix VOV; emulsion paint abraded and given one coat of Tekfix Primer; **existing vinyl** is allowed if completely sealed (no blown joins), hung with Tekfix VOV.
  - p.3-5: paste the wall; 30 mm selvedge for double cutting through; overlap lengths and cut through with a plastic strip protecting the wall; remove adhesive seepage.
  - Nothing in the guide says which of these surface and adhesive combinations the classified sample used, or that the fire class is retained on each.
- **Tekwrite hanging recommendations (4 pp, 2019):** Tekfix VOV adhesive recommended for all listed surfaces; plasterboard primed with Tekfix Primer; existing vinyl must be completely sealed; hanging surface must be "mirror smooth" (sheet, p.1). No fire condition stated.
- **Over existing wallcovering / lining paper:** only "existing vinyl" (completely sealed, Tekfix VOV) is addressed, in the digital guide and Tekwrite recommendations. Hanging over paper, over lining paper, or over other existing wallcoverings is not addressed: a text search of all downloaded files found no mention of lining paper (the only hits were film release liner and the phrase "lining up the pattern").
- **P3TEC wall protection** (not a wallcovering) uses Roman Pro 555 adhesive with Roman Pro 935 primer and says never to install over existing wallcovering; recorded only so that it is not confused with the wallcoverings.

## 6. Contradictions and outdated claims

1. **Sheet B versus page C.** Temple, Plaster Piece, Savoy, Stonewalk (sheets modified 21 August 2026, pages updated 24 to 25 August 2026): sheet "Euroclass B", page "Euroclass C". Ambient (sheet created 14 May 2026, page updated 24 June 2026): sheet "Euroclass B / Class 0 and 1 to BS476", page "Euroclass C". In the Ambient sheet the BS 476 part of the fire row is set in a different typeface from the rest of the row on the rendered page. Not resolved; no product-level class can be stated.
2. **Page shows more than the sheet.** Windward: page tags "Euroclass B" and "Class 0 and 1", sheet (2015 file) prints "Euroclass B" only. Brick, Wood and Stone (digital): page tags include "Class 0 and 1", sheet prints no BS 476 line.
3. **Page shows less than the sheet.** About 100 pages tag only "Euroclass B" where the sheet adds a BS 476 line; and for the B s1/s2 sheets the page omits the subclasses and shows "Euroclass B". The sheet is the more specific document, but neither is a classification report.
4. **Blanket claim versus sheet detail.** The Digital & Bespoke FAQ says "all products" printed on are "fire rated to euroclass b / class 0 & 1" (read as covering everything under "What we print onto"), but the same page lists acrylic as a print base and digital sheets print "Class 3 to BS476 part 7" for acrylic panels. The wallcovering sheets are the more specific source.
5. **Panel and film lines on the same PDF.** On the Geometrics PDF the window film (Euroclass B), the Zintra panel line ("Euro classification B when adhered to suitable substrate") and the wallcovering line use similar wording but are different products. The Zintra web page separately claims B-s2-d0 "in accordance with EN 13501-1" for acoustic panels, whereas the panel line on the sheets is plain B. These are panel claims and must not be carried to any wallcovering.
6. **Outdated or legacy claims.** BS 476 parts 6 and 7 "Class 0 and 1" appears on 110 standard sheets, Tekwrite sheets and 17 digital sheets, including files created in 2009 (Enigma, modified 2013), 2013 (Cityscapes) and 2014 (Books), and on new 2026 sheets. No year or report accompanies it. It is a legacy national classification; whether a particular project accepts it is a regulatory question outside this note.
7. **Digitally printed variants.** No classification evidence is tied to a particular base, ink, colour or print process: the sheet only says "when adhered to a suitable substrate", and the website says Tektura can print onto virtually any wallcovering in its range. No evidence covers prints on bases other than the vinyl base described on the sheet (called Digifort on other digital sheets and on the website).
8. **Typographical problems in standard references:** "Euroclass 13501", "EN3501", "EN13501".
9. **Sheets re-saved in 2026.** 64 of the standard sheets (created between 2010 and 2026) carry a modification date later than their creation date in 2026, 61 of them on 21 August 2026, and every one prints plain "Euroclass B" with no BS 476 line and no subclasses. Earlier versions are not available, so what the fire row said before is unknown. In the rendered pages of Suede, Metallix, Stonewalk and Ambient the fire text is set in a different typeface from the neighbouring rows. These are observations about the files, not proof of what changed. Meanwhile about 70 sheet files with 2026 metadata (for example Tiburon Shagreen, Patina Stone, Haven) still print the BS 476 line, so Tektura's own sheets are not uniform in 2026.

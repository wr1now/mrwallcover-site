# Newmor Wallcoverings: fire classification evidence (supplementary)

Research note for the wallcovering fire-ratings reference page. Checked 15 October 2026 (access date for every URL below). Included because Newmor publishes actual classification and test reports with the substrate and adhesive stated, which Arte's DoPs do not. SHA-256 values are recorded for each document because the download copies are temporary and are not stored in this repository. Machine-readable records: `F-arte-newmor.json` (agent F), products whose `manufacturer` starts "Newmor" (plus one record naming Len-Tex Corporation).

Scope rule: every rating below belongs to the named sample, construction and build-up in the cited document. Nothing here is a brand-wide statement about Newmor.

## 1. Summary

- **Newmor's library page (https://newmor.com/technicalinformation/) lists a "Fire Certificates" set of 12 files, a further 675 gsm fabric-backed certificate in its literature list, and 7 US fire certificates.** The EN 13501-1 documents come from three kinds of body: CNBOP-PIB (Polish notified body 1438), Warringtonfire/Exova/Bodycote (notified body 0833) and BTTG (UKAS-accredited laboratory 1066). They are report-level evidence for named samples, not DoPs.
- **Classes found:** B-s1, d0 (CNBOP reports for 460 g/m2 Premier fabric-backed and Premier nonwoven; Bodycote report for the 2009 "Oslo" paper-backed vinyl); B-s2, d0 (BTTG reports for 20 oz nonwoven, 400 gsm fabric, Mylar 460 nonwoven; Warringtonfire 675 gsm fabric-backed; Exova 380 g/m2 paper-backed); and a "probable B-s1, d0 but definite B-s2, d0" wording in three BTTG reports (350 gsm fabric, 350 gsm nonwoven, 400 gsm nonwoven).
- **Tested build-ups are narrow and different from site practice.** Test boards were fibre-cement (8 mm or 6 mm, 1800 kg/m3 where stated) or 12 mm calcium silicate board; adhesives were Dixon Turner Heavy or Light, or "Newmor Heavy". Where a field of application is stated, it requires an A2-s1,d0 or A1 substrate of high density; the Exova report and the CNBOP nonwoven report expressly exclude paper-faced plasterboard.
- **Several library labels do not match the documents they open.** The label "Fabric-Backed 400gsm (Paper-faced Plasterboard)" opens a 460 g/m2 fabric-backed report tested on fibre-cement board; the same file is also labelled "Type II Fabric-Backed 460gsm". "Fabric-Backed 400gsm (fibre-cement board)" opens a BTTG report tested on calcium silicate board.
- **Product spec sheets for named designs give only "Class B when bonded to a suitable substrate".** Savlamar and Eri Stripe sheets (460 gsm nonwoven Type II) print no smoke or droplet subclass and no report number. The "IMO" sheets are technical sheets, not IMO certificates.
- ASTM E84 results (US) are listed separately and are not EN 13501-1 classifications.

## 5. Contradictions and anomalies

1. **Library label vs document (plasterboard).** "Fabric-Backed 400gsm (Paper-faced Plasterboard)" opens CNBOP 081/BW/22: a 460 g/m2 fabric-backed vinyl tested on 8 mm fibre-cement board. The report does not mention plasterboard. The same file hash is behind "Type II Fabric-Backed 460gsm".
2. **Library label vs document (board).** "Fabric-Backed 400gsm (fibre-cement board)" opens BTTG 27/06068A, tested on 12 mm calcium silicate board.
3. **Two reports for a 460 g/m2 nonwoven Premier vinyl.** CNBOP 082/BW/22 gives B–s1, d0; BTTG 27/06093/02/23 gives B, s2, d0. The BTTG sample is described only as "20oz", so identity of the samples is not established.
4. **BTTG "probable" smoke wording.** Reports 27/05969A, 27/05969B and 27/06068B state a "probable Class B, s1, d0 but a definite Class B, s2, d0". For 350gsm the table shows s1; for 400gsm nonwoven the table shows s2. Normalised to s2; s1 is recorded only as probable.
5. **Exova 314369 adhesive.** The build-up table names Dixon Turner Light; the field of application names Dixon Turner Heavy.
6. **Warringtonfire 412883 field of application.** Unlike the older Warringtonfire reports, 4.3 names no primer or adhesive; they appear only in the build-up table.
7. **CNBOP 081 and 082 differ on plasterboard.** 082/BW/22 excludes paper plasterboard; 081/BW/22 does not mention it. Both leave the 75% density condition tied to a standard underlay density that they do not print.
8. **Oslo report exposure time.** Results are printed for a 15 s ISO 11925-2 exposure; the other Newmor reports state 30 s. Flagged as a question, not as an error.
9. **Surface iQ report.** The classified product names Len-Tex Corporation as manufacturer.
10. **Template text.** The CNBOP reports retain an italic instruction line about including a CE marking statement.
11. **Document age.** The Fiona (2008), Oslo (2009) and Exova (2012) reports are old but are still offered on the library page. No expiry is printed and none is assumed; the CNBOP reports state indefinite validity subject to no change; the BTTG reports state no validity.
12. **IMO labels.** "IMO Non-Woven" and "IMO Osnaburg" are technical sheets with no IMO or MED certificate number.
13. **Outdated BS 476 claims.** None found.

## 6. Gaps and questions for Newmor's technical team

1. Which classification document applies to a named design (for example Savlamar, Eri Stripe, Meadow)? The spec sheets print only "Class B when bonded to a suitable substrate" and the product table maps designs to backings only.
2. Is there any classification for a Newmor wallcovering on paper-faced plasterboard, or on painted plaster or lining paper?
3. What is the density of the "standard underlay" referred to in the CNBOP field of application, and do typical plasterboards meet "other underlays A2-s1,d0"?
4. Are "Dixon Turner Heavy/Light" and "Newmor Heavy/Light" the same adhesives?
5. Which smoke subclass should be quoted for 350gsm and 400gsm Premier vinyls (s1 "probable" or s2 "definite")? Are formal notified-body classification reports or DoPs available?
6. Are DoPs (EN 15102, system 3) available for Newmor products? None was found in the library.
7. Which label on the library page is correct for report 27/06068A and for 081/BW/22?
8. Are the older reports (Exova 314369, Bodycote 170849 and 177871) still current for supplied product, and does Newmor hold a re-issue under EN 13501-1:2018?
9. Not inspected and so not relied on: the Meadow sheet fire line, six US E84 reports (text only), and the Healthcare collection brochure.

## 7. Method note

Primary documents were fetched from newmor.com. PDF pages were viewed as images (a local renderer was used for pages where the built-in page renderer was unavailable) and cross-checked against extracted text. No document was altered, mirrored or copied into this repository. Verbatim extracts are limited to short phrases.

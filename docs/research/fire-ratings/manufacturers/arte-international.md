# Arte International: fire classification evidence

Research note for the wallcovering fire-ratings reference page. Checked 15 October 2026 (access date for every URL below). Documents were downloaded and inspected as rendered pages; SHA-256 values are recorded because the download copies are temporary and are not stored in this repository. Machine-readable records: `F-arte-newmor.json` (agent F), products with `manufacturer` starting "Arte".

Scope rule: every rating below belongs to the named product or product group in the cited document. Nothing here is a brand-wide statement about Arte.

## 1. Summary

- **Specification sheet 47253A is Moire Sauvage** (Collection Arte Contract, contract vinyl on non-woven backing, Type II, about 460 g/m2). It prints "Fire rating EU B-s2, d0" and "Fire rating US Class A". The fire line names no standard, no test report, no substrate and no adhesive. The sheet is a 2-page generated product sheet with no issue date.
- **The B-s2,d0 is supported by a DoP and a notified-body certificate**, but only for a stated build-up. DoP-CPR-24-3959 (21 June 2024) covers the 14 Moire Sauvage references and declares B-S2,d0 under EN 15102:2007+A1:2011. It cites Centexbel document 0493-CPR-W038, a Certificate of Constancy of Performance covering a family of Type II and Type III FR-treated vinyl wallcoverings of 451 to 790 g/m2, classified B-s2, d0 "Glued on Fibreciment, vertical joints". The adhesive is not stated. Nothing read extends the class to plasterboard, painted plaster or lining paper.
- **The "US Class A" evidence on the contract vinyl pages does not match the product.** The file Arte links as the US fire certificate is an ASTM E 84-12c report (Commercial Testing Company, report 13-03217, 26 March 2013) on a different product: a Koroseal digital substrate submitted by RJF International Corporation. It does not mention Moire Sauvage or any Arte reference. The Moire Sauvage data sheet does describe the US test conditions (GRC board and 5/8 inch type X gypsum board, both with A-848-B adhesive) but the underlying Moire Sauvage report was not found.
- **The contract vinyl range is not uniformly B-s2, d0 either.** Every Arte Contract product page prints B-s2, d0, but a Centexbel report for a mylar-faced contract vinyl (report 19/397, 10 December 2024, naming "Silk Raw Mylar 67502") classifies it C – s2, d0 and expressly excludes plasterboard from its field of application. The Silkx Raw DoP (21 June 2024) declares B-S2,d0 for 67490 to 67502, which includes 67502.
- **Arte's decorative range is not uniformly class B.** Centexbel reports and DoPs show B-s1,d0 (Type 01 non-wovens such as Le Corbusier Dots and Brushed Suede), B-s2,d0 (Type 03 vinyl on paper, Indienne), C-s1,d0 (Type 06D textile, Brocade and Ramage) and D-s1,d0 (Type 06C Collines, Type 06A Riverbank and Aranya).
- **Several Arte documents disagree with each other.** The most important: the Les Tapisseries Collines and Riverbank DoPs (2 April 2026) declare C-s1,d0, while the Centexbel reports they cite (17 June and 18 September 2026) and the web pages state D-s1,d0; the Aranya web page states C-s1, d0 while its DoP (2 September 2026) and the same report state D-s1,d0. See section 5.
- **Arte's Centexbel reports for the decorative range state a narrow field of application:** starch glue at 200 g/m2 (1 kg per 6 m2 for Collines) on end-use substrates of density 1350 kg/m3 or higher classified A2-s1,d0 or A1. Arte's hanging instructions and spec sheets instead recommend Arte Clearpro or a good quality dispersion adhesive on a prepared wall. The documents do not say whether the classification holds for that installation.
- IMO (marine) and ASTM E84 results are listed separately below and are not EN 13501-1 classifications.

### What the web pages print (manufacturer webpage statements only)

The Arte Contract landing page lists 42 designs. Each of the 42 product pages checked prints "B-s2, d0" and "Class A", with compositions "Contract vinyl on fabric backing (Type II)" (30 pages) or "on non-woven backing (Type II)" (12 pages), and the same 20 oz weight line. All of them link the same Centexbel CoCP file (row 5), the same IMO certificate (row 7) and, except Opaco, the same US report 13-03217 (row 6); the media identifiers were checked and are identical across pages. Eight pages (Arista, Belgian Linen, Meadow, Mistura, Opaco, Sambava, Sapphire, Tussah Silk) also link a "Fire-Certificate-Class-A-20oz-Mylar" file, which is a UL certificate for RJF International's "Type II-J" vinyl (row 31), again not an Arte-named product. Silkx Raw links the "7B" Centexbel report (row 30), which classifies its mylar article lower than the page states.

A sample of 48 decorative product pages (the listing page, not exhaustive) prints: B-s1, d0 for Type 01 non-woven, Type 02 natural and Type 06 textile families; B-s2, d0 for Type 03 vinyl on paper (Indienne, Ombre, Sculptura, Tali); C-s1, d0 for 3D 5 mm foam textiles (Memphis, Senah Lumera), for Brocade, Ramage and Aranya; D-s1, d0 for Collines and Riverbank. These are webpage statements and have not been checked against DoPs except where covered in section 3.

## 5. Contradictions and anomalies

1. **Moire Sauvage DoP: System 3 vs System 1.** DoP-CPR-24-3959 states System 3 and a "certification report Centexbel 0493-CPR-W038". The cited document is a Certificate of Constancy of Performance stating "under system 1". The classification matches (B-s2, d0).
2. **EN 15101-1 in DoP block 3.** The Moire Sauvage, Indienne, Les Tapisseries 06C/06D/06A (Riverbank) and Brushed Suede DoPs print "EN 15101-1" as the standard for use as wall covering, while block 8 of the same DoPs names EN 15102:2007+A1:2011. The Le Corbusier Dots and Mysuru DoPs print EN 15102 in block 3. I did not check what EN 15101-1 covers; the mismatch is flagged as probably typographical and unconfirmed.
3. **US "Class A" for Moire Sauvage.** The spec sheet, data sheet and web page state US Class A. The linked US file tests a different product (see summary). The Moire Sauvage data sheet's US test conditions (GRC board, gypsum board, A-848-B adhesive) cannot be traced to a Moire Sauvage report in the files read.
4. **IMO certificate manufacturer.** MED_2022_0010_B names Arte nv as applicant but Koroseal Interior Products, LLC as manufacturer, and the file is offered on the pages of all 42 Arte Contract designs. The certificate lists design reference ranges; Moire Sauvage appears on p.4. This is a marine approval and does not establish the building classification.
5. **Collines (06C).** DoP 00040/5 (2 Apr 2026) says C-s1,d0; cited report 18/667 (17 Jun 2026) says D - s1, d0; the web page says D-s1, d0. The report cites AITEX tests of 12 Apr 2026 and 21 May 2026, after the DoP date.
6. **Riverbank (06A).** DoP 00040/3 (2 Apr 2026) says C-s1,d0; report 24/2602 (18 Sep 2026) says D - s1, d0; the web page says D-s1, d0.
7. **Aranya (06A).** DoP 00050/2 (2 Sep 2026) says D-s1,d0 and cites the same report 24/2602; the web page says C-s1, d0.
8. **Brocade and Ramage (06D).** DoP cites report "20/1155"; the report on the product page is 20/1150. Classification C-s1,d0 agrees across web, DoP and report.
9. **Le Corbusier Dots DoP date.** DoP is dated 11 Apr 2022 but the version of report 19/721 now published cites AITEX tests of 29 Jul 2022, so the DoP cannot have relied on those tests. Brushed Suede article 59600 (web page) is outside the DoP reference ranges.
10. **American Flamecoat reports.** Types 1 and 3 share the same report number 108809 (both 14 Feb 2023). Type 6C cover is dated 23 Jan 2017 but its test report is dated 19 Feb 2018 and cites ASTM E-84-98 beneath a cover citing E-84-21. Type 6D cover cites E-84-21 while test pages cite E-84-98 and E-84-19, and its flame spread index is 25, at the upper limit of the Class A band.
11. **Report 24/2602 p.2** shows a stray "-04-03" and cites "AITEX: 2024AN1100 dd 2026-05-21" (reference year and date do not match). Report 19/720 cites "2019AN2798 dated 13-01-2025". Printed as found.
12. **Re-dated Centexbel documents.** Many Centexbel documents are dated June to September 2026 with first-issue or test dates years earlier (CoCP first issued July 2023 but signed July 2026). The relationship between issue date, re-issue date and the DoPs that cite them is not explained. No expiry period was assumed; the printed validity dates are recorded above.
13. **Silkx Raw mylar (67502).** DoP-CPR-20-3667 (21 Jun 2024) declares B-S2,d0 for references 67490 to 67502 and cites 0493-CPR-W038. Centexbel report 19/397 (10 Dec 2024, CREPIM test of 5 Nov 2024) classifies "Silk Raw Mylar 67502" as C – s2, d0, glued on A2-s1,d0 or A1 substrates above 652.5 kg/m3 "except plasterboard". The data sheet and web page print B-s2, d0 for the whole line. Also note that the density threshold in this report (652.5 kg/m3) differs from the 1350 kg/m3 used in the decorative reports.
14. **US "Mylar" file.** Eight Arte Contract pages link a UL certificate (R4616, 2012) for an RJF International "Type II-J" vinyl; it names no Arte product and gives flame spread and smoke indices without a class letter.
15. **Outdated BS 476 claims.** None found in any Arte document read.

## 7. Method note

Primary documents were fetched from Arte's own pdf and media hosts. The Read tool rendered each PDF page as an image; where text extraction and image differed, the image was used. Documents of ten pages or fewer were read in full. No document was altered, mirrored or copied into this repository. Verbatim extracts are limited to short phrases.

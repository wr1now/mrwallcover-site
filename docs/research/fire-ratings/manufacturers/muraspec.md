# Muraspec: fire-performance documentation for commercial wallcoverings

Research notes (agent C) for the Mr Wallcover reference page on wallcovering fire ratings. Checked on 2026-10-15 (our checking date). Manufacturer: Muraspec Decorative Solutions Ltd (UK; manufacturing in Kent). Source site: muraspec.com. Every claim below is attributed to a named document; nothing here is a statement about the Muraspec brand as a whole.

How to read dates and status in these notes:

- **Issue date** is the date printed on the document (signature or revision date). **Upload date** is when the file was added to Muraspec's media library. **Stated expiry** is only what a document prints (for the DoPs, the expiry of a notified-body certificate listed in the scope table, not of the DoP). **Checking date** is 2026-10-15.
- **Status** per product record: *current* means the document is hosted on Muraspec's site and nothing in the documents marks it withdrawn or replaced, and any overlapping document agrees with it. *unclear* means an overlapping or older document disagrees, or the document does not say what it covers, or only a webpage or data sheet supports the claim. Muraspec states no supersession rule in any document read.
- Class notation is kept exactly as printed, with a normalised version in the JSON file. Unknown smoke or droplet subclasses are left blank, not filled in.
- Reaction to fire (Euroclass) is not fire resistance. Nothing here describes Muraspec products as fireproof.

## 1. Summary

1. **Primary documents exist, but are not linked from Muraspec's Technical page.** The page lists hanging instructions, a cleaning guide and a lining paper data sheet only. Six single-page declarations of performance (DoPs) plus an older 2021 version are in the site's public media library: CPR0001 to CPR0005 signed 04/09/2024, a second CPR0003 signed 04/07/2025, and CPR0001 dated 01/01/2021. French translations of the 2024 set are also hosted.
2. **The declared class is tied to one build-up.** Each 2024 and 2025 DoP prints the reaction-to-fire result with the same condition: on fibre cement board using Murabond heavy adhesive. No DoP states a primer, lining, paint, other board, other adhesive, air gap, or any field-of-application or extended-application wording. The older 2021 DoP prints "B-s2, d0" with no build-up at all.
3. **The declared class is not uniform across the range.** Smoke subclass s2 and s1 both appear (B-s2, d0 in CPR0001, the 2024 CPR0003 and the 2025 CPR0003; B-s1, d0 in CPR0002, CPR0004 and CPR0005). The same product type carries different subclasses in different DoPs (paper-backed vinyl, Type II non-woven FBV, woven FBV). Muraspec has not explained which applies to a given roll.
4. **The notified body is not Warrington.** The word "Warrington" appears only in the file names of the 2024 CPR0001 and CPR0002. The documents name Elements Materials Technology, Rotterdam BV (no. 2812) for CPR0001/CPR0002/2021, BTTG (printed as "Shirely Techologies Limited") for CPR0003 to CPR0005 (2024), and Centexbel (no. 0493) for the 2025 CPR0003. No classification reports are published. The BTTG DoPs name laboratory report numbers; the Elements and Centexbel DoPs name none.
5. **Brand-wide "Class B" wording is marketing.** The explainer page and several blog posts say all products are Class B. Muraspec's own product pages state Euroclass C S3 D0 (19 acoustic foam vinyl pages) and "Euro Class C" (Glitter Jazz display fabric), and the DoP set has no entry for several product families (acoustic, self-adhesive film, grasscloth, magnetic).
6. **Installation documents are silent on fire.** The May 2026 hanging leaflet requires removal of existing wallcovering, specifies primers and adhesives by surface type, and gives one fire-related instruction (do not hang over expanded polystyrene wall veneer). It says nothing about the effect of lining paper, paint or an existing covering on the fire classification. Silence is not permission.
7. **No BS 476 claims were found** in any Muraspec document or product description read. IMO and ASTM E84 claims appear as assertions only (IMO certificates "on request"; ASTM E84 "Class A" on 2018 data sheets and acoustic pages), with no certificate or report published.

## 5. Installation conditions relevant to fire

All from the Hanging Information Leaflet, Version May 2026, doc no. 07660 (page numbers are the printed "Page x of 6"), unless stated.

- **p.1 checklist:** use only Murabond products in line with the pack instructions. Trial-hang three drops before proceeding.
- **p.1 General Surface Preparation, item 1:** remove all existing wallcovering and backing paper-backed vinyls (PBVs) and ensure the surface is clean, dry and sound. This is an adhesion instruction; the leaflet does not link it to the fire classification. Item 2 asks for a surface of uniform colour for lighter wallcoverings (emulsion or equalising paint).
- **p.1 Wall Surface Identification:**
  - uncoated absorbent surfaces (plaster, plasterboard, MDF): Murabond Primer diluted 1 part to 3 parts water, then the adhesive in the Product Information Notes;
  - coated absorbent surfaces (emulsion paint, some plasterboard coatings): rub down, Murabond Primer diluted 1:1;
  - multiple coats of emulsion may impair porosity; if in doubt, or if oil-based paint is beneath, treat as coated non-absorbent;
  - cement render, concrete and similar alkaline powdery surfaces: oil-based alkali-resisting primer, then treat as coated non-absorbent;
  - uncoated non-absorbent (metal, laminates) and coated non-absorbent (oil-based paint, plasterboard coatings): hang with Murabond Sealed Surface Adhesive.
- **p.1 Alternative Adhesive Systems:** Sealed Surface adhesive must be used on non-absorbent surfaces, applied one drop at a time. Murabond Heavy Adhesive must be used when hanging "Lining PBV" (a Muraspec paper-backed vinyl lining product, not the plain lining paper), diluted to the minimum workable consistency and hung as for absorbent surfaces.
- **p.2 General Hanging Recommendations, item 16:** "To minimize the hazard in the event of fire", do not hang wallcoverings over expanded polystyrene wall veneer, and make sure wallcoverings are firmly stuck down. This is the only fire-related instruction in the leaflet.
- **p.2 to p.5 Product Information Notes (table):** most FBV and non-PVC ranges list Heavy adhesive for absorbent surfaces and Sealed Surface adhesive for non-absorbent surfaces. Murek and Spectrum paper-backed vinyl ranges (p.3 Nuray; p.4 "all other Murek / Spectrum") list **Light** adhesive for absorbent surfaces. Acoustimousse and Buflon Mousse acoustic ranges (p.2, p.3) and the MemErase Magnetic (p.3) list Heavy / Sealed Surface.
- **Where the leaflet and the DoPs may not line up:** the DoPs name only Murabond heavy adhesive. The leaflet requires Sealed Surface adhesive on non-absorbent surfaces for nearly every range and Light adhesive on absorbent surfaces for some paper-backed vinyl ranges. The DoPs say nothing about whether those adhesives are covered, and the leaflet does not say.
- **Lining paper, existing wallcovering, painted surfaces:** no document read states that hanging over any of these voids or limits the fire classification, and none states it is covered. The leaflet requires removal of existing coverings (p.1) and gives primer and adhesive rules for painted surfaces (p.1).
- **Strataflex instructions (pp.1-2):** the film is for non-absorbent hard smooth surfaces; surface test, metal primer and oil-based wood primer on timber boards. No fire information.
- **Everglades instructions (1 page):** remove existing wallcovering; sand and wash painted surfaces; use undiluted Murabond Heavy. No fire information.
- **Muraspec Digital Terms and Conditions (legacy, p.2 section 10):** the seller accepts no liability for goods further processed after leaving its hands. A general sales term, not a fire statement.

## 6. Contradictions and outdated claims

Recorded, not resolved. No claim here is picked as "strongest".

1. **Brand-wide "Class B" against product-specific DoPs.** The explainer ("How do Fire Ratings work?", published 2023-10-23) says all Muraspec wallcoverings are Class B and gives "B - s2 - d0" as an example format. Other posts repeat it (2018, 2023, 2024, 2025). The DoPs declare B-s2, d0 and B-s1, d0 with a fibre cement board / heavy adhesive condition, and cover named product types only. The explainer mentions neither the condition nor any DoP.
2. **Same product type, different smoke subclass, different DoP.**
   - Paper-backed vinyl: B-s1, d0 in CPR0002 (2024, Elements) against B-s2, d0 for 220-300 gsm in the 2025 CPR0003 (Centexbel). The site tags all 152 paper-backed vinyl products CPR0003 and none CPR0002.
   - Type II non-woven FBV: B-s1, d0 in CPR0002 against B-s2, d0 (460 gsm) in the 2025 CPR0003, and CPR0001 (2024) declares B-s2, d0 for fabric faced non-woven FBV 460 gsm.
   - Woven FBV: B-s2, d0 for 360-460 gsm in CPR0001 against B-s1, d0 for Type I 360 gsm in CPR0004 (BTTG).
3. **Declaration number CPR0003 reused.** The 2024 CPR0003 (V1, BTTG) covers only digital print FBV 300/350 gsm. The 2025 document (V4, Centexbel) uses the same number for five product types including paper-backed vinyl and all Type I non-woven FBV. Product pages cite only "CPR0003"; their products (460 gsm FBV, paper-backed vinyl) match the 2025 scope, not the 2024 one.
4. **English against French CPR0001 (both V3 September 2024, signed 04/09/2024).** Three rows carry certificate 2812-CPR-KA0008 in English and 2812-CPR-KA0029 in French, and the 460 gsm row is worded differently (fabric faced non-woven fabric backed vinyl in English; textile covering on non-woven polyester backing in French). The 2021 version lists KA0029 as the non-woven metallic type.
5. **Certificate labels on product pages against DoP scope.**
   - Self-adhesive film (Strataflex) pages cite CPR0001; no CPR0001 version lists such a type.
   - Acoustic foam vinyl (Buflon Mousse) pages cite CPR0001 yet state Euroclass C S3 D0; no CPR0001 type is acoustic.
   - A Muralon page (13602) cites CPR0004 and shows 460 gsm; CPR0004 names 360 gsm only.
   - The Glitter Jazz page says it is not a wallcovering, states "Euro Class C" and cites CPR0001.
6. **Webpage class statements with no document.** "Euroclass B fire rated" appears on the Motifs Asia, Scandi and Amazonia pages and the Magnetic wallcovering (also Lookbook 2025 p.31); the page text gives no smoke or droplet subclass, no build-up and no DoP. The Lookbook 2025 icon legend (p.2) says "All our products are fully compliant" beside a "Fire Rated" icon; p.32 says all marine products are "flame retardant".
7. **2018 data sheets against the DoPs.** The four digital print base sheets (September 2018) print "Euroclass B-s2, d0" and ASTM E84 "Class A" with no build-up. The 2024 and 2025 DoPs attach the fibre cement board / heavy adhesive condition to B-s2, d0. The 2021 DoP also has no condition. It is not stated whether the 2018 sheets and the 2021 DoP are superseded; both remain hosted.
8. **Printed digital products.** A 2016 post says the digital range is Euroclass-rated "even when printed". The DoPs name digital print bases by weight (300/350 gsm; "including digital products" 270-460 gsm in 2025) and say nothing about print coverage, UV ink or bespoke artwork. Several newer digital product pages show "IMO Rated: Yes" and no CPR reference.
9. **BTTG notified-body details are printed inconsistently.** The name is spelled "Shirely Techologies Limited"; a Dublin address sits under the UKCA label and a Manchester address under the CE label; the numbers are printed as "UKCA 0338 2024 and CE 2895 2024". We did not check these against any register. The DoP file name for CPR0001 and CPR0002 says "Warrington" while the document names Elements Rotterdam; the DoPs do not name a test laboratory in Warrington or anywhere else.
10. **Certificate expiry dates close to the checking date.** Printed notified-body certificate expiries of 29/11/2026, 16/12/2026 and 15/03/2027 follow our checking date of 2026-10-15 by weeks to months. The DoPs do not say what applies afterwards. We have not assumed any DoP is invalid.
11. **BS 476, IMO and ASTM E84.** No BS 476 (Class 0 or Class 1) claim was found in any document or in a search of product descriptions. The explainer refers only generally to a pre-2013 national class method. IMO claims ("IMO Rated: Yes"; "IMO & USCG Rated: Pass" on the 2018 Marine sheet) have no published certificate (TC-4198 says the certificate is available on request). ASTM E84 "Class A" appears on the 2018 data sheets and the acoustic pages without any report.
12. **Hanging leaflet adhesives against the tested adhesive.** See section 5.
13. **The explainer's Table 1** is an image (generic Euroclass classes against test methods) served from a legacy "/pub/media" path. It is generic and carries no product data. It describes Class B as very limited contribution to fire and Classes D and E as acceptable contribution.

## 8. Method and limits

- **How the DoPs were found.** The explainer links no documents and the Technical page links no DoP. The DoP file names were found in the site's public WordPress media library (read-only REST search) and each PDF was then downloaded directly from muraspec.com/wp-content/uploads. Product-page certificate labels and counts were read from the same public site data and cross-checked on sampled pages.
- **Rendered checks.** PDF pages were rendered to images and read, in addition to the text layer, for all DoPs except the French CPR0003 to CPR0005 (text layer only), for the leaflets and data sheets, and for the full bespoke brochure. The Lookbook 2025 was checked rendered at pp.2, 31 and 32 only.
- **Not verified.** Notified-body identities and certificate status in any official register; the standards themselves (EN 13501-1, EN 15102, EN 13823, EN ISO 11925-2); whether the EU Construction Products Regulation wording in these DoPs gives them standing in Great Britain (left to the regulatory research); whether any DoP is withdrawn; the contents of the three inaccessible brochures.
- **Jurisdiction.** The DoPs cite Regulation (EU) 305/2011 and, for the BTTG versions, both UKCA and CE markings. Muraspec is UK-manufactured. The notes make no claim about legal sufficiency in England, Wales, Scotland or Northern Ireland.
- **Machine-readable records.** `C-muraspec.json` (17 product records, 63 source records, 15 open questions) was written to the shared research notes folder and is not part of this repository.

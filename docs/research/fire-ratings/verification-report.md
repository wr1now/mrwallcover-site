# Independent verification report: wallcovering fire ratings reference page

Reviewed 15 October 2026 by an independent AI reviewer that did not write the page. **This is an AI review. It is not professional fire-engineering approval, it has not been checked by a chartered fire engineer or a building-control body, and it should not be presented as such.** It tests the evidence and the public wording against the primary documents; it does not decide what any project needs.

**Primary documents reopened.** Rendered page images (Read tool, via the `pdfpages` helper) for the regulatory tables: Approved Document B Volume 2, 2019 edition with 2020/2022/2025/2026 amendments and forthcoming 2029 changes (`AD_B2_2026.pdf`, printed pp. 54 to 60, 138 to 141 and the 2029 Appendix B pp. 152 to 155); Welsh ADB Volume 2 (printed pp. 102 to 104); Scottish non-domestic handbook April 2026 (pp. 133 to 135); Northern Ireland Technical Booklet E (printed pp. 76 to 79); Arte's US ASTM E84 file; Newmor/CNBOP report 082/BW/22 (all 5 pages, a scan); Warringtonfire report 22965F p. 2. **Live re-checks (15 Oct 2026):** the GOV.UK Approved Document B collection record (last updated 16 September 2026, Volume 2 title and amendment list unchanged); the 12 BSI Knowledge records for the standards table (metadata only); all 93 URLs in `sources.json` (all answer 200 or 206); Muraspec product page BM-61527103 and its "How do Fire Ratings work?" article; Vescom's Colour Choice+Protect page; Tektura's Temple, Rivulet and Tiburon Shagreen pages (rendered in the browser pane, because they are scripted).
